Strategy

If the slime is out of range to attack, it will use Bowling Roller. If it is in range to use Gluttony, it will do so. It will use Digestion when it can on the turn after it hits with Gluttony.

Running this monster

A solo monster against which regular attacks will do little. A group with heavy magic damage may be able to kill it normally, but dealing damage without landing a critical hit is nearly impossible. Make sure to give the players enough hints and guidance so that they can realize this.
