Strategy

The bandersnatch starts with Dire Claw at the start of its turn. If it cannot reach any enemies without moving or if it can finish off an injured target, it will use Mana Razor Alpha.

The rest of its abilities are used immediately when they become available.

Running this monster

A single bandersnatch is a challenging encounter for a party.

Gather Parts: 2 claws, each containing 2000 units of mithril. Can be gathered from twice, once for each claw.
