Manticore

The manticore is a monstrous combination of three different types of animal originally fused together through magic. They possess little intelligence and do little more than feast on anyone unfortunate enough to run into one.

A large creature with the body of a lion, a scorpion like tail and a large pair of wings.
Habitat
Can be found anywhere in the wild or in a dungeon.

Strategy

The manticore will open with Lion's roar. If it managed to slow any targets, it will focus on that target with its crushing maw. If no enemies were affected by the roar or if there's no enemies in range to attack, it will use tail spikes. Afterwards, it wil attack with the tail.

Running this monster

The manticore can be run together with 3 other heroic creatures to make a good challenge.

Gather Parts: 500 units of direhide.
