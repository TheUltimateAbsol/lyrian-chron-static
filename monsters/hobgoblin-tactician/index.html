Strategy

The hobgoblin tactician uses its Stratagem: Lightning at the start of the encounter. When it loses more than 15 HP, it will use its Gobbo Regeneration.

The hobgoblin tactician is smarter than other hobgoblins and can move tactically to hit weaker enemies. It may also choose to take some damage early and save RP to be able to heal the damage up with its self healing.

Running this monster

The hobgoblin tactician is best matched with several hobgoblin fighters The tactician can speed up the fighters and get them in range. It uses a 2handed weapon for extra damage.
