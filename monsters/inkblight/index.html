Strategy

The Inkblight moves forward hitting the target that looks the weakest. If targeted by an attack they can dodge, they will use their Inky Dodge to do so.

If they are in a position to use their Ink Attack, they will do so and use the strongest attack possible.

Running this monster

A group of 16 inkblights are a suitable threat for an experienced group of 4 adventurers. Inkblights, like most fiends, are intelligent. They will target squishy looking targets and use their inky dodge to pressure ranged attackers with the teleport.

Recommended spirit core level: 2000-2500.
