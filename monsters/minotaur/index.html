Monster · Heroic Humanoid

Minotaur

A creature often confused with the chimera of Northi. Despite a similar appearance, the original minotaur is a cursed creature once locked away in a labyrinth. Its offspring now roam the world, causing destruction wherever they go.

Strategy

The minotaur will use all their AP on Goring Charge if not in range of an enemy. Once in range, they will attack normally and use Headbutt when they can.

Running this monster

A group of minotaurs provide a decent challenge for a group of adventurers. Its stats are fairly high, but its abilities are unimpressive and it mostly just basic attacks.
