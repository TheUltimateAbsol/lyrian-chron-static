Strategy

The Rime Vermin uses Self Icicle Nail to get in range if it can. When in range to attack, it will use Heavy attacks if it can and otherwise will just use Light attacks.

Running this monster

A large group of around 16 rime vermin makes a challenging fight for a group of 4. You can replace 4 of these to replace them with a heroic unit that might benefit from the ice the rime vermin create. 

Gather Parts: Using gather parts nets a Rime Vermin Ball, which functions like an Elemental Mixture Flask (Frost) and is worth 200 Clim.
