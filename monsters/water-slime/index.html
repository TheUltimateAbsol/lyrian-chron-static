Running this monster

About 4 slimes per player would make an adequate challenge for parties. Parties with only physical damage will struggle against slimes.

Gather Parts: 250u of Vivinthal flamellons. With a check of at least 20 may also choose to gather the core, which functions as a Rare Water Herb.
