Monster · Heroic Humanoid

Troll

Trolls descend from what were once regular humans that mutated into these hideous creatures. 

In a way, they are similar to what hobgoblins are to normal goblins. Perhaps because of this, they are often found in the company of them.

Strategy

The troll simply attacks the nearest target. It should only react to Heavy attacks.

Running this monster

A group of trolls provide a decent challenge for a group of adventurers. Its stats are fairly high, but its abilities are unimpressive and it mostly just basic attacks.
