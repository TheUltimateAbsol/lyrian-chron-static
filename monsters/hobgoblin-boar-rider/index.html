Strategy

The boar rider will charge towards the enemy backline, aiming to trample as many enemies on the way there as possible.

Running this monster

A large group of boar riders will make a decent challenge for a group of adventurers. As they are a mounted enemy, boar riders excel in open fields and are worse indoors. They can also be combined with other Hobgoblin enemies for a more varied challenge.
