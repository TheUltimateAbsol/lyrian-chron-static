Strategy

It's very adaptive, e.g. it will try to counter its opponent's actions. However, it doesn't plan ahead, it simply reacts. It just wants food, and will try to escape death. Peaceful resolution may be possible with sufficiently large offerings?

Running this monster

One giant crab is a good challenge for a group of 4 SC3000+ characters.
