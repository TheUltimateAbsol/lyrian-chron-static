Drake Wyrmling (Fire)

A distant relative of the dragons that have vanished. The wyrmling is a young wyrm that has not yet matured. Despite their age, they are not any less hostile and vicious - attacking any unknown creature that approaches it.

Strategy

The wyrmling will stay at range when it can and use its Firebolt ability to weaken the enemy. When engaged in melee, it will fight as normal.

Running this monster

A large group of wyrmlings will provide a decent challenge for a group of adventurers. They can also be used together with their stronger relatives like the drake and wyrm.
