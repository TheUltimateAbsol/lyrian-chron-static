Strategy

Zombies move towards the near enemy. If the target is not already grabbed (by any zombie) and they can make a Heavy attack, they make a Heavy attack. Otherwise if they are not already grabbing an enemy, they will do so.

Running this monster

A large group of zombies is a decent challenge for a group of adventurers. Zombies are weak to holy damage, so you will want to add more of them to keep the challenge the same when the players have classes like a paladin or priest with them. Zombies are slow, but relatively tanky for a grunt enemy. Against parties that are less likely to exploit this weakness or if fighting in a place where its impossible to do, you may want to use slightly less zombies.
