Monster active action

Bandersnatch Swipe

0 AP

Range: 5ft

Keywords: Daze

You swipe at the nearest enemy and toss them 30ft away in a direction of your choice. The target passes through allies for this movement, but will collide with walls. If they hit a wall, they take 1d4 true damage and are dazed until the start of your next turn.

Requirements

Can only be used while your HP is at 25% or lower.

Used by monsters
