Monster active action

Shards of Ice

1 AP

Range: 60ft

Keywords: Frost,Full Pierce,Spell

You fire a shard of ice at every enemy within range, making a Light attack against them that deals Frost damage. This attack gains full-pierce against any enemy that is slowed.
