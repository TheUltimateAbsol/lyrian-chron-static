Monster active action

Headbutt

0 AP

Range: Melee

Keywords: Combo

On dealing damage with a melee attack, you can follow-up with a headbutt as a Light attack. On damage the target is Unbalanced.

Requirements

Landing a normal Light or Heavy attack
