Monster active action

Poisonous Bite

1 or 2 AP

Range: 5ft

Keywords: Rapid,Poison

You make a Light (1 AP) or Heavy (2 AP) attack against the target. On damage, the target must succeed on a save or become Weakened until the end of their next turn. A character that is already weakened takes an additional 5 (Light) or 10 (Heavy) full-pierce Poison damage instead.
