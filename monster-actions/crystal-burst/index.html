Monster active action

Crystal Burst

0 AP1 MP

Range: 120ft

Keywords: Sure Hit

You make a Light attack that deals Astra damage to all enemies in range. You also take that amount of true damage and your max HP is lowered by that amount. If this reduces your mana to 0, you explode and deal an additional Sure Hit & Full Pierce heavy damage to all enemies in range, after which you die. When you die in this way, you do not drop a fiend core.

Requirements

Your absorb magic did not trigger since the end of your last turn.

Used by monsters

MonsterCrystal Behemoth
