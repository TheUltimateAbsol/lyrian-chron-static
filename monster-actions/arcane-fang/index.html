Monster active action

Arcane Fang

1 AP

Range: Melee Weapon Range

Keywords: Daze

Your bite channels internal magic. You make a Light Attack that deals Arcane damage. On damage, the target becomes Dazed. If the target is already Dazed, this attack instead deals an additional 8 full pierce Arcane damage as the lingering magic intensifies.
