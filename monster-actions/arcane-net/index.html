Monster active action

Arcane Net

1 AP1 MP

Range: 60ft

Keywords: Spell,Arcane,Root

You place a 20x20ft net of arcane energy at a point within range. The net is considered difficult terrain for your enemies. You may spend 0 RP to cancel the net, immediately rooting all creatures within the net’s area of effect at that time until the start of your next turn.

Used by monsters

MonsterMage
