Identity & lore

Class overview

Lancers are a class that specializes in using polearms.

Tactical guidance

Playing Lancer

Lancers prefer to let the enemy come to them. The full pierce effect gained from their Brace key ability makes even a readied light attack do good damage. 

Extending Spear allows the lancer to attack from long range and hit a lot of enemies if they line up. Extending Spear followed by a readied attack is a good way to start fights and wait for the enemy to get closer.

Once they are close, a crescent sweep can knock them all off their feet and set them up for the rest of the team. 

When there is only a few enemies, look for a position to use Piercing Stab in. 2 AP for 2 heavy attacks is very cost efficient, even if you have to move for 1 AP to position for it.

Finally, lunge with a polearm gives you a 20ft attack range. This is long enough to hit basically every enemy in the front line and may be enough range to even hit a backline target that moved up too far.

Key ability

Brace

Class feature

Benefit 1

When using the Prepare action to prepare a melee attack with a polearm, if your target of that prepared attack moved more than 20ft that turn (excluding teleports), your attack gains Full Pierce and does maximum damage. Against a mounted enemy this also gains Sure Hit and on damage, their movement becomes 0 until the start of your turn.

Benefit 2

On mastering this class, the Sure Hit property (but not the movement nullification) of your prepared attack against mounted enemies now also applies against Beast type enemies that are large size or bigger.
