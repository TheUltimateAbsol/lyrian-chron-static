Identity & lore

Class overview

Mycomancers are powerful mages that harness the power of fungi to heal allies and destroy enemies. They try to understand death through mycology.

Tactical guidance

Playing Mycomancer

Mycomancers are a unique healer that relies on dealing damage to heal their allies. The only target they can heal is their Spore Symbiote. 

Once it is placed, you can use Saprophytic Bolt to heal your target. Grunts may react to it even if its a light attack, so burning their RP first may be advised so you don't waste it.

Destroying Angel is a bigger heal and also comes with the benefit of instantly killing the target when used against grunts. It is not worth using against non-grunts however.

Poison Spore is the last ability that can center around the spore symbiote. It can be used as a zoning tool of sorts, but be aware that your allies are not immune to the poison. One of the better uses is placing it around yourself or the symbiote if they are a more vulnerable target and using the spore as a deterrent to any assassin type enemies that might threaten the backline.

Amoxicillin is a simple disease removal spell. The cast time makes it unusable in battle and you are required to identify the disease first, but it's a free removal once you do.

As a mycomancer, it is recommended you heal early rather than late. Your healing can be unreliable and you don't want your allies to drop low, only for you to miss a crucial a heal and watch them go down.

Ability

Poison Spore

2 AP1 RP
Range
Self or The Host of Poison Spore. 15ft x 15ft Area with the target at the center.
Keywords
Concentration,Poison,Full Pierce

You may spend either 2 AP or 1 RP to use this ability.

You create an Area of Poison Spores around you or the Host of Spore Symbiote. Units who end their turn in the Poison Spore take Full Pierce Poison damage equal to your Power + 2. You and the host of Spore Symbiote are immune to your own Poison Spore. Poison Spore lasts until the end of the encounter but you can only have one instance of Poison Spore active at a time.

The spore stays centered around the original target, even if the target of this ability moves. If the original target of this ability was the symbiote target and you swap the symbiote target, the location of the poison spore moves with it.
