Identity & lore

Class overview

Fate was a strange thing. Nothing could stop its inevitable march. Time moved forward, even if it could be nudged and manipulated. Destined actions could be nudged, too. A thread here, a thread there, and the fated fall of a hero could be nudged further down the temporal road. Nudge it enough and that hero might be able to live a full life and pass of old age.

Or so her master had told her.

Vereana bit back a curse as she ducked under a searing beam of magical energy, hearing it scorch through a tree somewhere behind her. A swarm of glittering, gleaming shards burst from her hands and engulfed the enemy mage that had been trying to take her out. He shouted at the sudden, blinding magical crystals surrounding him but it gave her the chance to focus on something that wasn’t her own safety. Master had never talked about dodging swords, arrows, and magic missiles in his training.

She flicked her fingers and threads of crimson suddenly appeared, wrapped around her hand and extending over to Benilus. For a moment, she saw double. A fate in which Benilus was not quite fast enough with raising his shield. He still caught most of the heavy blow but the angle of his shield allowed part of it to slam into his shoulder, staggering him to his knees. But with a nudge of mana down the strings, fate twitched and changed. The surge of energy brought swiftness to Benilus’s shield arm and he caught the blow cleanly, letting it slide perfectly off his block and into the dirt.

He retaliated in that moment of over extension, driving the berserker back. 

With her other hand, she reached out and grasped the shining green threads that wrapped around critical points of his body, sending a surge of energy through them until they cut. The cascade of magic still reached him, reinvigorating him with the potential of lost moments of chance.

Atropos, her master called it.

She had made Master angry when she insisted on calling it “Healing Strings”.

But Master hadn’t been wrong. Those tiny moments, little nudges, turned the tide of the fight. Benilus overcame his opponent and was able to push on to help their allies. Without someone holding their front line, the mage that had been attacking her had no time but to focus on his own survival, freeing her up to send in her own magical attacks and attend to more threads in support of her friends.

They were one step closer to meeting the fate of dying of old age and she was going to do her best to make sure they all found that ending.

Thread of Reprieve

1 AP
Granted byThread of Reprieve
Range60ft
Keywords
Active

You apply the Threads of Reprieve to a single ally until the start of your next turn 

If that ally takes damage while the threads remain attached, you may spend 1 mana to heal them 3+Focus, up to the damage they took.

Ability

Thread of Defiance

1 AP
Range60ft
Keywords
Active

You apply the Threads of Defiance to a single ally until the start of your next turn that grants them a +2 bonus to saves.

If that ally fails a Save while the threads are attached, you may spend 1 Mana to grant them a bonus to that save equal to your Focus.

Ability

Thread of Deliverance

1 AP
Range60ft
Keywords
ActiveAid

You apply the Threads of Deliverance to a single ally until the start of your next turn that increases their Dodge by 2 and Block by 4. 

While the threads remain attached, the first time an ally takes the Dodge action, but fails to avoid the attack or takes the Block action but fails to fully block the attack, you may spend 2 mana to make them regain 1 RP.

Ability

Thread of Triumph

1 AP
Range60ft
Keywords
ActiveAid

You apply the Threads of Triumph to a single ally to reduce their critical hit range by 1 (IE: you crit on an 19-20 instead of a 20) until they score a critical hit.

If the target scores a critical hit while the threads remain attached, you may spend 1 RP to give them haste.

Ability

Atropos ~ Sever the Thread

1 RP2 MP
Range60ft

You cut the threads of fate you attached to an ally, removing a single Fateweaver thread from them and granting them an effect.

The ally gains an effect based on what thread was cut:

Reprieve: The target is healed for 6+2 x your Focus

Defiance: The target gains a bonus to their next save equal to your Focus.

Deliverance: The target regains 1 RP.

Triumph: The target gains Haste

Requirements

An ally within range has one of your threads attached to them.
