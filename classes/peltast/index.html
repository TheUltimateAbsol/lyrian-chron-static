Identity & lore

Class overview

A specialist in using throwing weapons.

Tactical guidance

Playing Peltast

The peltast makes throwing weapons a valid choice for a main weapon. The boomerang key ability makes it so you no longer have to keep paying AP to draw more weapons. The peltast ideally wants to play close to the enemy and isn't afraid of melee combat.

Long Toss gives you a long range option. The range of throwing weapons is normally a bit low, so this can be used when you would be out of range to attack normally.

Rappelling Dagger is a movement option that can also deal damage. Useful when you have to engage an enemy that's not too far away and it can be used to obtain high ground by rappelling into a tree or on top of a building.

Fan of Knives is a point blank AoE that can be used even when engaged in melee. It will require you to get close to the enemy before you can use it.

Stab & Throw is an AP efficient attack that requires you to be in melee, plus to have another enemy nearby.

Key ability

Throwing Rhythm

Class feature

Benefit 1

When you make an attack with a throwing weapon, or take the thrown attack basic action, you may move 5ft as if you had the light sword passive (Nimble).

Benefit 2

When you take the thrown attack basic action, the weapon returns to your hand afterwards.

Ability

Stab & Throw

3 AP
RangeSpecial

You may make a Heavy Attack against a target in melee range. You may then use the same weapon to Heavy Attack a different target within your ranged weapon range or thrown attack range. This second attack does not provoke attacks of opportunity from the target of your first attack.

Requirements

Throwing Weapon, Set of Missiles or Thrown attack only.
