Identity & lore

Class overview

“Move along, sir. Move along.”

Renaud waved the merchant through the checkpoint absently, his eyes not leaving the opposite set of gates. Tables ran half the length of either side of the checkpoint, staffed on one side by humans and on the other side by demons. Officials representing Westria and Sorthen, checking travel papers, wares, and business. In theory, Jav was under the control of Westria, having changed hands during the war once again. In practice, the city had always been mixed and an inner wall and multiple checkpoints linked the human half of the city to the demon half. 

It was more complicated than that, but that was the basics of it. Plenty of humans (and others, like chimera) lived on the demon side and there were demons that freely lived on the human side. And as much animosity had been born between humans and demons during the war, Jav had always had its way of doing things and they rarely followed how their respective nations behaved. Still, this was one of the only locations on the Westria-Sorthen border to cross into each nation easily and that brought all types.

He knew them well and his demon compatriots on the other side knew them as well. A couple times a year, someone from one side or the other would try something. A rush through a checkpoint, an alchemical bomb, or some sort of magical attack. Normally handled quickly and with little fuss as those that chose to take those sorts of actions couldn’t fathom that the human and demon guards would work together to stop them.

A family of catfolk stopped briefly in front of him, presenting their signed travel papers. His eyes flicked over them with an automatic swiftness of someone who had done this thousands of times.

“It’s in order, move along,” he told them.

They had just cleared the gate when he heard a shout from behind him.

“Renaud!”

He turned, his shotgun coming up on automatic at the urgent shout. A young man was sprinting toward the gates, his hands tucked close to his chest, holding something. Citizens were already running or moving out of the way and he knew the other guards in the checkpoint would be reacting soon enough. But he was the closest.

“Halt!”

The young man looked at him in shock and then anger but didn’t stop his sprint.

BOOM!

The cobblestone around the runner’s feet shredded into chunks and he skidded to a stop, just barely missing having his legs blasted out from under him by the shotgun blast. He’d still caught a ricochet of it.

“Remain where you are! Raise your hands above your head!”

The young man stared at him and at the smoke rising gently from the muzzle of his gun.

“W-why are you doing this?!” he demanded. “You-you are a man of Westria! You should be helping me cleanse these-these monsters!”

Renaud narrowed his eyes as he cautiously stepped forward. If he had to guess, this kid was Euphoric Front…or believed he was. And now that he wasn’t running, Renaud could see he was carrying a pair of flasks, one with a gleaming silver liquid and the other with a burning orange one. Catalyst bomb. Break both flasks into each other and that much volatile mixture could blow a hole in the wall…or kill dozens of people trapped inside the checkpoint.

“On your knees! Now! I will not tell you again, son.”

The other guards were keeping their distance and ushering the crowd away. He saw it in the kid’s eyes the moment before the kid even realized he’d made the decision. A wildness, nearly madness, burning like a flame.

“I’ll burn the impure--” he raised his hands to smash the flasks together.

BOOM!

One flask tumbled to the cobblestone and shattered, the volatile fluid hissing as it rapidly evaporated. The kid’s hand landed next to it with a wet slap. He started shrieking, dropped the other flask, and was promptly tackled by a demon guard with gleaming silver wings and a cracked halo over her head. She may have cracked his head against the cobblestone a bit harder than necessary but at least he wasn’t screaming any more.

“Nice shot, Renaud. Maybe call this kid a healer, yeah?”
