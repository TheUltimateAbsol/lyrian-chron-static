Identity & lore

Class overview

Angelblooded are humans with angelic heritage that can transform into semi-divine beings.

Tactical guidance

Playing Angelblooded

The main appeal of the angelblooded is the True Divine Release. Not only does it give flight and extra movement speed, it is also a requirement to use the other two abilities of the class. Unfortunately, the upkeep and cost make it hard to use until you obtain God's Blessing, which reduces both to 0.

Once this class is mastered, it becomes very powerful. On top of the benefits mentioned above, you get to make a free light attack every round. You also get to do a stronger block every round, making blocking an option even if your toughness is low and you're not wearing armor.

Even after mastering it, it is still a once per arc ability. Make sure you don't use it too early.
