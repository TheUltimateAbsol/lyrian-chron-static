Identity & lore

Class overview

Kiraran rogues who use a mix of physical prowess and magical arts to slay their enemies in the darkness.

Tactical guidance

Playing Ninja

The ninja is a slippery ranged class that uses throwing weapons.

Their first ranged option is the Kibaku Fuda Shuriken. It's a fairly AP efficient attack, even if used against just a single target, but it is of course ideally tossed into a group of enemies.

The Kage Shuriken no Jutsu is used against single targets. Unless they target chooses to block the first one, you are guaranteed that at least one of the attacks hits, making it good against dodgy targets.

A lot of power of the ninja comes from their Bunshin Tai no Jutsu. It creates a copy that shares everything with the original body. It can be used in many different ways. For example, you can use the body double to threaten enemy ranged attackers. You don't need to worry about overextending too much. If the enemy decides to retreat and collapse on you, you can simply dismiss the copy and escape from harm. You can also use it to flank enemies and hit them from where cover won't protect them. The important here to keep in mind that is that you are never overextended with a clone. If you feel you are in danger, simply dismiss it.

Another powerful defensive tool Ninjas have is Kawarimi no Jutsu. The RP cost is higher than normal, but it protects even against sure hit attacks and because you can hide and move, you are also safe from any future follow up attacks.

Ability

Bunshin Tai no Jutsu

1 AP2 MP
Range
5ft
Keywords
Ninjutsu,Stealth

You create a body double of yourself that has the same stats you do at the time of creation. You and the body double share the same HP, Mana, AP and RP etc and unless stated otherwise your clones are considered you. All effects on you are mirrored on your clone and vice versa, however a clone cannot benefit from Presence Concealment.

When taking an action, you may choose to let the user be yourself or the body double. However, the body double may only use basic abilities and abilities with the Ninjutsu keyword and cannot create more body doubles of itself. 

When you take the movement action or use a non-teleport movement ability, your clone may move for the same amount of distance as you moved.

You may spend 0 AP or 1 RP to dismiss the body double. A clone can never travel more than 300ft from your actual body, but while it lasts you may spend 2 AP to swap locations with it.

A clone lasts until you rest.
