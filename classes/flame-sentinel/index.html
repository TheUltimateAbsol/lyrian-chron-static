Identity & lore

Class overview

A supportive fire magic user that can buff their allies and zone away enemies.

Tactical guidance

Playing Flame Sentinel

Flame Sentinels support their allies primarily through offense. Flame Weapon grants a small power boost to a weapon of an ally and lets them deal fire damage, which may be important against some enemies. Controlled Blaze will not prevent your allies from setting things on fire, so be careful when giving it out when flameable material is around.

Flame Shield allows you to do damage after an ally takes damage. It costs 1 RP, but its fully unavoidable and full pierce damage. If you hang out in the back and don't have much use for your RP, this can be an excellent way to spend it.

Flame Infusion is a situational ability. Against certain enemies it's extremely powerful and against others, its a detriment.

Flame Wall is a strong zoning tool, able to cut off a large portion of a fight. You can also use it as a damage tool, dropping it right on top of enemies to force them to use an RP. If they choose not to do so, or if they have no more RP to spent, then it is effectively a Sure Hit attack once their turn starts.

Ability

Flame Infusion

1 AP0 MP
Range30ft
Keywords
FireSpellType

You surround a willing target in searing flames, changing their typing to Fire II. As a Fire II type, they take half damage from Fire and Frost Damage or attacks with the Fire or Frost keywords unless that attack deals true damage. They take 50% more damage from Water Damage or attacks with the Water keyword. While the ward lasts, they also gain immunity to Burning. The ward lasts until the end of the encounter or until their type is overwritten by a more powerful type.

Ability

Flame Wall

2 AP3 MP
Range60ft
Keywords
ConcentrationFireSpell

You create a wall of Fire that is 30ft long, 5ft wide and 30ft high. This wall must be a straight line. Anyone passing through the wall or starting their turn in it takes Heavy Fire Damage. The wall lasts for 10 minutes or until canceled. Those in the wall when it is casted, may spend 1 RP to sidestep out of the wall. A person can only take this damage once per round.
