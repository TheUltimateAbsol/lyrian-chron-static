Identity & lore

Class overview

While Transmuters provide foundations for everyday life and artisan work, Alkahests are those who are obsessed with the subtleties of matter and creation.  Alkahests are the experts in the fields of Transmutational Alchemy, quickly altering reality with precision and artistic flair.  They're able to immediately concept and transmute just the right tool for any situation.

Historically, one Alkahest above all others was a pioneer in her field.  She remains celebrated across Lyr to this day—Etoile Dacquoise.  While her best friend and rival, Riannon Aquanas, focused on the art of core Alchemy, Etoile dedicated her life to mastering the complexities of Transmutation.  In the atelier together, these pioneers had opened an often historically referenced shop in the Kingdom of Mothergreen—and became the foundation of innovation during Lyr's darkest hours.

Historical word of mouth speaks of Etoile's contributions from behind the scenes in the battle against Heira, the Goddess of Evil.  Although she never wielded a sword on the front lines, her mastery of transmutation alongside Riannon's expertise in alchemical creations created the tools and solutions needed by their friends that did.  The members of those legendary heroes helped create that shop themselves, as the procurement arm of the business.  Etoile's legacy is still immortalized to this day behind the statues of Riannon and the other heroes:

Elly, The Hero
Uruka, The Sylph
Signum, The Heretic
Leaflit, The Pixie
Brynhildr, The Dragon
Basalt, The Dwarf

Today's Alkahests follow in Etoile's footsteps and in her teachings, fulfilling their roles as vital supporters and adventurers, heroes and innovators alike.  They sometimes provide assistance from the sidelines, demonstrating that heroism is not only about direct confrontation but also wits, innovation and dedication to a future victory.

Tactical guidance

Playing Alkahest

The Alkahest is a crafter and utility class with very limited combat potential.

Your best combat ability is your Secret Art: Transmute Proto Armatae. While it costs a fair bit of mana, you can view the armatae's HP as getting a ton of bonus HP that stacks with temporary HP. In addition, you get to make a free attack each turn. It doesn't increase your damage output much (or at all), but it's good defensively. You can use it defensively by spending RP and absorb a hit with the free 20 HP shield it comes with.

Alchemical Detonation is your other ability that can be used in combat, although it is situational. Strong when you're against enemies that use wall creation. It also has strong out of combat benefits, allowing you enter virtually any building at little cost.

Transmute Effective Implement is another out of combat tool that can grant you a massive bonus to a skill check. If you're willing to spend enough mana, you can succeed at almost any check if you want to.

Siphon Materials costs an interlude point, but it lets you extract valuable materials that might otherwise be wasted in an item you can't use. You could extract the silver from a katana to have a blacksmith make it into a dagger for example.

Finally, stabilize matter is another co crafting ability. It statistically beats out the other options, though you do have to precast it instead of using it after you see the roll. A small bit of creation lets you convert a herb to one of a different element at the cost of reducing the rarity. Using this reduces the value of herb, so it should only be done if you desperately need the herb.

From the surrounding material you construct an “Effective Implement”, granting you a +5 to the skill of your choice.  You must explain how this implement will be effective towards the check using the relevant skill (example:  creating a springboard to jump higher with athletics) and the GM determines the validity or this ability has no effect.  Does not work with crafting or gathering skills. This implement lasts until you have attempted the specific check it was created for. 

You may also spend 1 RP instead of 1 AP for this ability.

Secret Art

Secret Art: Transmute Proto Armatae

2 AP4 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Secret Art

You touch the ground, transmuting a Proto Armatae of your own design.  The Proto Armatae lifts you up and immediately encases you into its cockpit.   The Proto Armatae is 15ft tall and is a large creature that occupies a 15ftx15ft space.  All creatures and moveable objects surrounding the Proto Armatae are pushed back away from its zone of control in a burst of steam.  The Armatae also gains a temporary shield with 20 HP that disappears at the start of your next turn.

You must use your own actions to cause the Proto Armatae to move and attack.  You can eject from a transmutation hatch on the top at the center of the Proto Armatae for 1 AP, in which the Proto Armatae stops functioning.  When you re-enter you must close the hatch for 1 AP before the Proto Armatae functions again. 

The Proto Armatae has health equal to 15 + 5x Focus and it uses your AP and RP for actions.  You can channel your Transmuter abilities through the Proto Armatae, and Transmute Weapon can create a giant sized weapon for the Armatae to use.

You may also spend 1 RP to use this ability instead of the AP cost.

Only usable once.  You regain the use of this ability when you gain an Interlude Point.
