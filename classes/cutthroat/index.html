Complete rules for every ability granted by Cutthroat.

Key ability

Surgical Precision

Class feature

Benefit 1

When you deal damage with a one-handed melee weapon that is both Sure Hit and Full Pierce or a critical hit, you inflict Bleeding on the target for 1 turn. This effect stacks with other Bleeds effects and itself up to 3 times.

Ability

Exposing Stab

2 AP
Range
Melee Weapon Range

You make a Light attack against the target. On damage, you may use your Devastating Strike against the target until the end of your turn as if the target had 0 RP.

Ability

Feinting Stab

2 AP1 MP
Range
5ft

You make a Heavy attack against the target. If they react to it in any way, you may cancel your attack on them and instead make it against a different enemy in range. Any RP spent by the first target is not refunded.

Requirements

At least two enemies within range.

Ability

Devastating Strike II

1 AP
Range
Melee Weapon Range
Keywords
Sure Hit,Full Pierce

You make a Light attack against the target. The dice are maximized instead of rolling.

Requirements

The target has no RP.

Ability

Artery Cut

1 RP
Range
Melee Weapon Range

You change the arc of your weapon and go for a vital spot, turning the hit into a critical hit.

Requirements

One-Handed weapon only, can be used after a light attack hits and is not reacted against.
