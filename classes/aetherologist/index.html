Identity & lore

Class overview

“Aetherology.” Professor Lina’chi’s porcelain-like finger tapped the desk for emphasis. “The study of and manipulation of Aether through the lens of mathematics. Quantifiable, repeatable, organized.”

Her eyes scanned her class, a collection of all kinds of races from humans, chimera, a couple goblins, and even a dwarf, gauging their attention. She raised her hand and a circle of symbols and formula of gleaming magic spun from her fingertips. None of them were particularly awed by it, she noted, but Annwyn was full of aethercraft and such formula circles were relatively commonplace. Her formula flared and the blackboard behind her was suddenly populated by notes in her neat writing, graphs, and dissected glyphs.

“In this course, we will be exploring the basics of aetherologic manipulation, glyph construction, and formula. This is not,” she tapped her finger again for emphasis, “a class for blind manipulation of aether through feel or emotion. Consider this your warning. You are all trained mages and I will not stand for such disruptive chaos in my class. If anything about that offends you or is beyond you, the door is there and I wish you a safe journey to Elyswyn. Where you can learn to get in touch with your feelings and instincts with the rabbits.”

She waited for a few moments. A rabbitfolk girl near the back of the class was glaring at her but remained seated. “Good.”

“We will begin with a fundamental. The Aether Barrier. All of you know how to invoke it, yes? In one form or another. Today, you will learn the details you have passed over with feeling and intent.”

She flicked her fingers again and the board behind her shifted to a complicated diagram of a formula circle. She let her students see the aetherological representation of Aether Barrier for a minute before she swung her hand. Glyphs, symbols, connecting lines, binding edges, and more erased themselves from the board. What was left was a plain circle containing a perfectly symmetrical triangle, inside which three more circles were contained, overlapping evenly with each other in the center.

“Pandora’s Gift. The fundamental foundation of all aetherological formulae. Remove all definitions, boundaries, and connections from any formula and you will find this at its base. Study it, understand it, memorize it. For while we all walk in the footsteps of Pandora into the wide world of discovery and the unknown, we all begin our journey here.”

The scratching of pens, quills, and pencils filled the room as students wrote hasty notes and copied the diagram. In varying degrees of accuracy, she noted. But the skills to draw even shapes would come with practice and time. Besides, now she got to relish in the memory of yet another class’s stunned and enamored expressions when they learned about Pandora’s Gift. Another class, another group, that finally understood something a little more deeply about the Goddess of Magic.

Secret Art

Secret Art ~ Final Theorem: Unanswerable Equation

2 AP
Range120ft
Keywords
SpellSecret Art

You unleash the Final Theorem making a Heavy attack that deals Arcane damage against the target.

This ability gains bonuses based on your three theorem levels:

Power:

  • For each level of Power, add another 1d6 to the damage.
  • For each 3 levels of Power, add your Power to the damage again.

Expanse:

  • For each level of Expanse, the radius is increased by 5ft. It gains additional bonuses based on level reached.
  • 1: This turns it into a 5ft radius AoE and you can mark a point instead of a target.
  • 3: You may choose to exclude allies you can see from the effect.
  • 5: The range of this ability increases to 720ft.
  • 7: The attack ignores all cover except full cover.

Inevitability:

  • For each level of Inevitability, gain +1 accuracy to the roll. It gains additional bonuses based on level reached.
  • 1: The attack gains Half Pierce
  • 3: The attack gains Lock-On
  • 5: The attack gains Full Pierce
  • 7: The attack gains Sure Hit
