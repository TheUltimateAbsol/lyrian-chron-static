Ability

Expertise Study (Armorsmithing)

No listed cost

You gain 5 Expertise Points in 2 different armorsmithing crafts. You also gain wielding proficiency in Heavy Armor or Greatshields.

In addition, you always gain the expertise bonus when crafting an armor for yourself. An armor crafted this way cannot be worn by other creatures and is effectively valued at 0 clim.

Ability

Spiked Armor

1 AP1 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Counter

Until the start of your next turn, the spikes on your armor and shield reflect damage when you are attacked. When an enemy hits you with a melee attack, they take 4 full pierce damage.

Requirements

Must be wearing armor and/or a shield.
