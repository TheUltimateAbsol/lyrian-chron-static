Glide

1 RP
Granted byWind Master
RangeSelf
Keywords
Spell

You slow down your falling speed to 15ft per round and you are able to move horizontally as if normally moving. If you hit the ground while Glide is active, you do not take any falling damage and this effect ends.

Gale-Wind Aura

2 AP
RangeSelf
Keywords

You project a Gale-Wind Aura around yourself that increases the movement speed of allies (excluding yourself) within 60ft of you by 20ft. An ally that starts their movement in the Aura, keeps the Speed increase for that move even if they leave the aura. Upkeep: You must spend 1 AP at the start of each turn or the aura fades.

Headwind

2 AP
RangeSelf
Keywords
AuraUpkeepSlow

You project an aura around you that hinders the movements of enemies. 

All enemies that start their movement within 30ft of you become slowed. If an enemy takes a Move basic action outside of your zone and spends more than 1 AP, they are still slowed after calculating each individual AP spent on the move.

Upkeep: 1 AP

Ability

Fly

1 AP2 MP
RangeTouch
Keywords
SpellConcentration

You or the creature you touch gains Fly, which gives them controlled Flight, Hover and their movement speed is doubled while flying until the end of the encounter. If they take damage while flying (not hovering), their altitude drops by 20ft. 

If this would reduce their altitude to the point where they touch the ground, they become rooted and cannot fly again until the end of their next turn. If the attack that reduced your HP came from an flying creature other than yourself, you do not lose altitude.
