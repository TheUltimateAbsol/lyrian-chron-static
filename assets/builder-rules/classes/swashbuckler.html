Benefit 1

When attacking a target inflicted with Offstep, Stagger or Blinded, you gain a +1 accuracy bonus against them.

Ability

Staggering Slice

1 AP1 MP
RangeMelee Weapon Range
Keywords
Stagger

You make a Light attack against the target. On damage, you inflict Stagger 1 until the end of your next turn..

If you're under the effects of Exposing Edge, you inflict Stagger 2 on damage instead.

Requirements

One-Handed weapons only.

Ability

Deckbreaker

1 AP2 MP
RangeMelee Weapon Range
Keywords
OffstepLock On

You make a Light attack against the target. On damage, you inflict Offstep on the target for the next attack against it.

If the target is inflicted with Stagger, the mana cost of this is reduced by 1.

If you're under the effects of Exposing Edge, this attack gains Lock On.

Requirements

One-Handed weapons only.

Ability

Dust Kick

1 RP1 MP
Range5ft
Keywords
Blinded

You kick the ground in front of you, causing a veil of dust to appear. The target becomes Blinded 3 until the attack resolves. If the triggering attack misses, you kick the target and may push it 5ft into any horizontal direction.

If under the effects of Exposing Edge, you apply Blinded 5 instead.

Requirements

Must be within 5ft of the ground. An ally or you are being attacked by an attack from enemy in range.
