Discern Immunities

0 AP
Granted byDiscern Immunities
RangeSight
Keywords
Quick

You look at a single target and glean what its resistances are until the end of the encounter. When you are about to use an ability with a negative effect that would be passively negated by the target, you become aware of this and may cancel your action.

Does not apply if the target has an active ability that can remove it, such as a paladin’s Paragon of Virtue for example.

Ability

Pinning Shot

1 AP2 MP
RangeRanged Weapon Range (Up to 60ft)
Keywords
Root

You make a Light attack against the target with your weapon. On damage, if there is a wall or similar object directly behind the target, they become pinned against it. A pinned target is considered Rooted until they spend 2 AP to remove the arrow pinning them.

Requirements

Bow or Crossbow only.
