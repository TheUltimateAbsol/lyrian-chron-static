Ability

Heavenly Lightning Stance

0 AP1 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Stance

You enter the Heavenly Lightning Stance, a stance that lets you move with the unpredictability of the heavens. For your first movement of your turn, you may pass through enemies and your movement becomes a Sidestep.

If you have mastered Sky Talon Style, entering this stance also counts as your Wind Stance.

This stance lasts until you enter a different stance or make an attack with anything other than a melee attack or an ability with the Unarmed Strike keyword.

Ability

Lightning Lariat

2 AP1 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Unarmed StrikeOverchargeComboTrick Attack

You gather lightning energy and move up to your speed in a direct line and make a Heavy attack against a target. If you moved more than 60ft before making the attack, your attack gains the Trick Attack keyword.

Overcharge: You may spend X mana to move an additional 2 x X times your speed. 

Combo: If used after Heavenly Lightning Stance or Secret Art: Lightning Release, the mana cost is reduced by 1.

1 RP

Secret Art

Secret Art: Lightning Release

0 AP3 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Secret ArtTransformation

You infuse yourself with Lightning and gain Lightning Release until the end of the encounter, allowing you to deal Lightning damage with your unarmed attacks and immediately enter the Heavenly Lightning Stance. While this lasts, you also gain the following benefits: 

  • Your movement speed is doubled.
  • You can make an additional attack when you use Double Punch. 
  • When making an unarmed attack, your critical threshold is lowered by 1 (19-20 base). Whenever you hit with a critical hit with an unarmed attack that deals Lightning damage, the attack arcs to a different enemy within 20ft of the original target. This attack uses the same attack roll and damage, but cannot arc again.

If you ever leave the Lightning Stance, you lose the benefit of this.
