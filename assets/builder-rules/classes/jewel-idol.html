Key ability

Cool Spotlight

Class feature

Benefit 1

Your Idol songs gain +15ft range. This stacks with other key abilities that increase their range.

Benefit 2

Your One Beat, One Heart song does not count towards your song limit.

Benefit 3

When paying upkeep for One Beat, One Heart, you may spend 1 mana to double its effect.

Ability

Never Miss The Beat

2 AP
Range30ft
Keywords
SongSoundIdolUpkeep

You begin singing a supportive song.

Once per round per ally, when an ally fails to meet the base evasion with an attack, you may spend 2 Mana to add that ally’s focus bonus for that attack.

Upkeep: 1 AP

Ability

Until My Voice Breaks

No listed cost

Whenever you’re about to pay mana on one of your abilities with the Idol keyword, you may instead reduce your current and max HP by 8 for each mana you’d spend.

This max HP reduction lasts until the end of the encounter and cannot be removed.

Ability

Offbeat Recovery

1 RP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Lock OnHalf RapidAugment

You give your ally’s attack Lock On, but the damage is minimized (and cannot be maximized) and it loses Full Pierce, Block Crush and any on hit or on damage effects. If used after a target has already dodged, they may Block against that attack for free.

Can be used when an ally misses an ability to make it not count as a miss for your Secret Art: Full Combo (but the attack does not actually gain Lock On)

Requirements

An ally within your Idol Song’s range misses a basic attack.
