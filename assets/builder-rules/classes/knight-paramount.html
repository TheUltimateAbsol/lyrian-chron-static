Key ability

Living Cover

Class feature

Benefit 1

While mounted, any ally within 5ft of you may use you as Low Cover. If an attack would miss them because of your cover benefit, the attack instead hits you.

Ability

Blocking Move

1 RP
RangeSelf

You move in front of the target and their movement action immediately ends. Until the end of their turn, they are Challenged by you and your next Block action against them costs 0 RP. If they willingly move from you through any method (including teleports) you may make a Heavy attack against them with Sure Hit.

Your movement may ignore Line Movement restrictions for this ability.

Requirements

You made an opportunity attack against a target moving away from you. Must be mounted.

Ability

Heavy Trample

No listed cost

When using Trample, you may use your Toughness for the damage roll instead of your mount’s power.

Ability

Armored Blow

2 AP1 MP
RangeMelee Weapon Range

You make a Heavy attack against the target. Your next Block against that target costs 0 RP and gains a +10 bonus.

Requirements

May only be used after moving at least 20ft in the previous action. Must be mounted.

Secret Art: Unstoppable Onslaught

RangeSelf
Keywords
Secret ArtSure HitMind AffectingFearChallengeShaken
