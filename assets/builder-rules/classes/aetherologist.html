You prepare to unleash the Final Theorem.

The area becomes saturated with magical energy, immediately alerting all in the combat. While this is active, you can never enter the hiding state and the first Thaumaturge spell you cast each turn raises your Theorem level.

You may at any time decide to stop preparing the Final Theorem for 0 RP. If you do, all stacks are lost. Stacks otherwise last until consumed by the Final Theorem or until encounter conclusion.

Secret Art

Secret Art ~ Final Theorem: Unanswerable Equation

2 AP
Range120ft
Keywords
SpellSecret Art

You unleash the Final Theorem making a Heavy attack that deals Arcane damage against the target.

This ability gains bonuses based on your three theorem levels:

Power:

  • For each level of Power, add another 1d6 to the damage.
  • For each 3 levels of Power, add your Power to the damage again.

Expanse:

  • For each level of Expanse, the radius is increased by 5ft. It gains additional bonuses based on level reached.
  • 1: This turns it into a 5ft radius AoE and you can mark a point instead of a target.
  • 3: You may choose to exclude allies you can see from the effect.
  • 5: The range of this ability increases to 720ft.
  • 7: The attack ignores all cover except full cover.

Inevitability:

  • For each level of Inevitability, gain +1 accuracy to the roll. It gains additional bonuses based on level reached.
  • 1: The attack gains Half Pierce
  • 3: The attack gains Lock-On
  • 5: The attack gains Full Pierce
  • 7: The attack gains Sure Hit
