Energy Drain

0 AP2 MP
Range60ft

You deal Light full pierce dark damage to a willing ally other than yourself. This damage ignores damage resistance against dark damage (such as from gloom infusion or corvuskite armor), unless that DR is an universal type of DR.

You gain Haste, which gives you 2 AP for the rest of this turn. A target can only be affected by haste once per turn.

Secret Art

Secret Art: Spirit Stigma

0 AP
Range60ft
Keywords
Secret ArtSpellDark

You stigmatize the targets spirit, they are inflicted with Spirit Stigma until the start of your next turn. A target with Spirit Stigma counts as a willing ally for your Eremancer abilities and damaging them fulfills the Malevolent  Phantasm requirement.
