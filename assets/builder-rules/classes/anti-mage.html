Ability

Dispel Magic

2 AP3 MP
Range60ft
Keywords
ArcaneDispelSpell

You dispel a temporary magic effect from an area or target. All non-permanent effects within range that are created by an ability with the Spell or Enchantment keyword are affected. Spells that do not have a time limit listed or create a permanent effect such as a Geomancer's fissure are not affected. Bounded Fields and Secret Arts cannot be removed with Dispel Magic.

Anti-Magic Shield

1 RP
Range60ft
Keywords
AidArcaneShieldSpell

You grant the target ally 1 RP that must be spend on a basic Dodge or Block action against the triggering attack as well as Anti-Magic Shield for the triggering attack. Anti-Magic Shield reduces the damage the target ally takes by your Focus.

Ability

Counterspell

1 or 2 RP
Range60ft
Keywords
ArcaneCounterSpell

You pay RP equal to half the AP spent on the triggering Spell (rounded up) or equal to the RP spent on the triggering spell and Counter it, canceling its effect without refunding the AP. If the AP cost is higher than your RP, you cannot Counterspell it. 

If used against a non-Grunt target, you must pay 1 mana for each point of Focus that the target has more than you (minimum 0). 

This Spell does not provoke attacks of opportunity, but cannot be used to Counter Spells that have an AP and RP cost of 0.

Requirements

An enemy casts a spell within range
