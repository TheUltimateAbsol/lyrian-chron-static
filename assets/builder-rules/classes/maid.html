Ability

Allow me to clean that~

1 AP1 MP
Range5ft
Keywords
Rapid

You instantly remove a negative effect from the terrain (such as Oil Slick, Sheet Ice, Web etc). Permanent effects such as fissure or effects with HP like a wall cannot be removed with this. A maximum of 15x15ft can be removed in 1 use and you may move to any spot of the cleaned terrain afterwards.

In addition, you may target an ally with this to remove 1 negative physical effect from them instead.

Ability

Overtime again?

No listed cost

When you take the Heal Injury, Appraise or Mentor IP actions, you may do the same action again for an EP.

In addition, if you take the Rest interlude action you may give the benefit to a different character instead. This does not stack with any Rest bonuses they may have already.

Ability

Snack Time Everyone!~

2 AP1 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Upkeep

You present a tray of refreshing Health and Mana snacks to your allies. All allies within 5ft of you may spend 0 RP to eat a snack.

Health: The ally may spend 2 Mana to heal for 15 HP.

Mana: The ally recovers 1 Mana. This counts as a character’s one food for the day and a character cannot benefit from this ability if they already had food. Does not count as a meal for the purposes of fending of starvation.

You keep the tray of snacks up for 1 turn and a character walking within 5ft of you while it’s up may also spend the 0 RP to eat a snack. 

Allies may also spend 0 AP to eat another snack. They can do this once per turn.

Upkeep: 1 Mana, you keep the tray up for an additional turn.

Requirements

At least 1 hand free
