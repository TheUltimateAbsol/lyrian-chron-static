Key ability

Forager's Path

Class feature

Benefit 1

You gain proficiency in Giant Scissors and proficiency in your choice between Axes or Hori. You may use axes for harvesting foraging outcrops.

Benefit 2

You gain the ability to harvest Tier 1 Foraging Outcrops.

Benefit 3

You gain the ability to use natural item as replacements for an Adventurers Kit and Torches such as: using the sun/moss as a compass, using vines as rope etc. The material must be available (GM’s discretion)

Benefit 4

You gain the ability to create Wood Blocks as an Errand. See item: Carpenter Materials. You may use an IP to do this 4 times instead.

Ability

Verdant Instinct

1 Strike Dice Other

Make a gathering check and apply it to NP. After rolling, roll two more dice that will be used as your next 2 strike dice values. You may reorder them or replace one with a 5.

Requirements

Must be in a gathering session.

Ability

Lifebeat Sense

1 AP1 MP
Range30ft
Keywords
ExposeAnalyze

You can assess the approximate HP percentage of the target until the end of combat.  (The DM should enable health bars for the unit, or you can ask for an approximate percentage). In addition, the target is afflicted by Expose 1, reducing the target’s crit threshold by 1. (20 to crit becomes 19.) Expose is removed once the target is critically hit (regardless of if they defend or not)

Ability

Divining Petalfall

1 Strike Dice Other

 Make a gathering check with a +5 bonus. If the final dice value is even, add it to Node Points. If it’s odd, add it to Lucky Points.

Requirements

Must be in a gathering session.

Ability

Vivisect

3 AP1 MP
RangeMelee Weapon Range

You cut the target in half with your scissors or chainsaw, killing them instantly.

Requirements

Giant Scissors or Chainsaw only. Target is affected by Expose and is a grunt.
