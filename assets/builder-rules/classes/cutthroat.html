Key ability

Surgical Precision

Class feature

Benefit 1

When you deal damage with a one-handed melee weapon that is both Sure Hit and Full Pierce or a critical hit, you inflict Bleeding on the target for 1 turn. This effect stacks with other Bleeds effects and itself up to 3 times.

Exposing Stab

2 AP
RangeMelee Weapon Range

You make a Light attack against the target. On damage, you may use your Devastating Strike against the target until the end of your turn as if the target had 0 RP.

Ability

Feinting Stab

2 AP1 MP
Range5ft

You make a Heavy attack against the target. If they react to it in any way, you may cancel your attack on them and instead make it against a different enemy in range. Any RP spent by the first target is not refunded.

Requirements

At least two enemies within range.
