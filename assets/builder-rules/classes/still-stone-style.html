Key ability

Shielding Fist

Class feature

Benefit 1

While you have at least 1 hand empty and aren't wielding a shield, you gain a +4 Shield bonus to your block.

Ability

Steadfast Earth Stance

0 AP1 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Stance

You enter a stance that grants you the toughness of the earth. You gain a +2 bonus to your guard and a +6 bonus to your Block.

This stance lasts until you enter a different stance.

Requirements

Not wearing any armor (including armor plates)

Ability

Hardening Fist

1 AP1 MP
RangeMelee Weapon Range
Keywords
Unarmed StrikeCombo

You make a Light attack against the target. Afterwards, you gain Hardening Fist, which gives +2 guard and +8 Block, but lose 4 evasion (including dodge) until the start of your next turn.

Combo: If used after Steadfast Earth Stance, the mana cost is reduced by 1.

Ability

Earthen Guardian

1 RP2 MP
Range40ft
Keywords
SidestepJump

You jump and sidestep towards the attacked ally and the triggering attack resolves against you instead. You automatically take the Block action against this attack.

Requirements

In Steadfast Earth Stance, ally within range is the target of an attack.

Secret Art

Secret Art: Jade Skin

0 RP2 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Secret ArtDisarm

You take the Block action against the triggering attack. If you reduce the damage to 0, the attacker becomes Disarmed until the start of their next turn.

Requirements

You are attacked by a Melee attack.
