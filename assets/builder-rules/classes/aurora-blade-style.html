Key ability

Frost Weapon

Class feature

Benefit 1

When attacking with a weapon, you may choose to have the weapon deal Frost Damage instead of its normal damage type. You must declare you chosen damage type before making the accuracy roll.

Ability

Aurora Blade Style: Winter's Edge

2 AP1 MP
RangeMelee Weapon Range
Keywords
SlowFrost

You imbue your blade with frost energy and make a precise attack that deals Frost damage. On hit, the target is slowed until the end of your next turn.

Requirements

Light swords, longsword, katana or dueling weapon only.

Ability

Aurora Blade Style: Aurora Slash

2 AP1 MP
Range80ft
Keywords
SlowFrost

You fire slashes in a straight line creating a curtain of light which strikes the first target hit with a light attack that deals frost damage. On hit the target is slowed until the end of your next turn. 

The light from the slash continues for the full range, creating a 300ft high wall that blocks vision on both sides. This wall of multicolored light last until the end of your next turn.

Requirements

Light swords, longsword, katana or dueling weapon only.

Ability

Aurora Blade Style: Rimestep

0 RP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Sidestep

When a target is slowed by an attack with the frost keyword, you may sidestep up to your speed towards the target. If you have enough movement you can end your movement anywhere next to the target. Overcharge: You may spend 1 Mana to instead make it three times your speed.

Secret Art

Aurora Blade Style Secret Art: Permafrost Guilotine

0 AP
RangeMelee Weapon Range
Keywords
Secret ArtStanceFrost

You make a light attack against the target or spend 1 extra AP to make a heavy or precise attack that deals Frost damage. On damage, the target is rooted until the end of your next turn.

Then, enter Permafrost Guillotine stance. While in this stance you can use Aurora Blade Style Secret Art: Permafrost Guillotine again. The stance lasts until the end of combat or until you enter different stance.

Requirements

Can only be used after you move to a target using Rimestep. Light swords, longsword, katana or dueling weapon only.
