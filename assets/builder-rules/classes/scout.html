Key ability

Independent Actor

Class feature

Benefit 1

When working alone (No known allies within 300ft of you) you gain a +10 bonus to Perception, Insight, Survival and Stealth checks. Must be alone for at least 15 minutes to gain this benefit.

Benefit 2

You gain a +5 bonus in Art (Cartography) expertise. This is not doubled for expertise bonus.

Benefit 3

You may use Scout’s Retreat.

Benefit 4

Having an Aetherie or similar connection active automatically fails the ‘No known allies’ requirements.

Range300ft
