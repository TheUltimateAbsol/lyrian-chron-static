Transmutation Tricks

Class feature

Benefit 1

For 1 AP, You can create small items using transmuting to provide temporary comforts and curiosities— a cup to drink out of, a chair to sit down on, a figurine etc. These items cannot have significant gameplay factors (such as a key or a tool). If you’d like to create something small to give away or sell, you must utilize x amount of commodities of whatever quantity and type the GM decides.

Benefit 2

You may use your transmuter abilities a second time during a craft or co craft.

Ability

Transmute Effective Implement

1 AP1 MP
Range5ft
Keywords
Overcharge

From the surrounding material you construct an “Effective Implement”, granting you a +5 to the skill of your choice.  You must explain how this implement will be effective towards the check using the relevant skill (example:  creating a springboard to jump higher with athletics) and the GM determines the validity or this ability has no effect.  Does not work with crafting or gathering skills. This implement lasts until you have attempted the specific check it was created for. 

You may also spend 1 RP instead of 1 AP for this ability.

Overcharge:  You can spend 1 additional mana to increase the skill bonus to +10.

Alchemical Detonation

1 AP
Range5ft
Keywords
Rapid

You touch a wall, door or container in front of you, which shatters from deconstructive force, becoming destroyed.  Can destroy up to a 5ftx5ft segment of wall.  Only affects objects made out of common building materials (wood, clay, stone, iron etc), however it affects all walls made out of player abilities.  The GM determines if the object is detonateable.  You can then take a movement action to move 5ft into the space where the wall was.

Ability

Siphon Material

No listed cost
Keywords
InterludeErrand

Errand. You siphon the alloyed material from an item if possible, removing the effects that the original item would’ve had. If you cannot remove the effect then the skill fails. 

You remove the alloy and regain its original form, while also maintaining the form of the item minus the effect. The special material is retrieved in raw units.

Secret Art

Secret Art: Transmute Proto Armatae

2 AP4 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Secret Art

You touch the ground, transmuting a Proto Armatae of your own design.  The Proto Armatae lifts you up and immediately encases you into its cockpit.   The Proto Armatae is 15ft tall and is a large creature that occupies a 15ftx15ft space.  All creatures and moveable objects surrounding the Proto Armatae are pushed back away from its zone of control in a burst of steam.  The Armatae also gains a temporary shield with 20 HP that disappears at the start of your next turn.

You must use your own actions to cause the Proto Armatae to move and attack.  You can eject from a transmutation hatch on the top at the center of the Proto Armatae for 1 AP, in which the Proto Armatae stops functioning.  When you re-enter you must close the hatch for 1 AP before the Proto Armatae functions again. 

The Proto Armatae has health equal to 15 + 5x Focus and it uses your AP and RP for actions.  You can channel your Transmuter abilities through the Proto Armatae, and Transmute Weapon can create a giant sized weapon for the Armatae to use.

You may also spend 1 RP to use this ability instead of the AP cost.

Only usable once.  You regain the use of this ability when you gain an Interlude Point.
