Unlocked ability

Fae Knight Style: Willow Stance

0 AP
Granted byKnight of Yggdrasil II
RangeSelf
Keywords
QuickStance

You enter Fae Knight Style: Willow Stance. Increasing your Fluttering Blade Limit to 2. Shields can be used as Fluttering Blades while in Willow Stance. 

For 2 mana, all Fluttering Blades created in Willow Stance this turn can be enhanced with Heavy Spin, allowing them to be used for some Willow abilities but they no longer gain your agility as extra evasion.  Heavy Spin blades also deal 5d6 upgraded heavy attack damage, even if they were originally a one-handed weapon.

When directing your Heavy Spin blades to move, you may direct two blades for 1 AP instead of just 1 blade.

Ability

Fae Knight Style: Carapace

0 RP
RangeSelf

One of your Fluttering Blades returns to you and interferes with the attack. You may take the dodge or block action against the attack. 

Your Fluttering Blade needs LoS to you or the target it is assisting.

This cancels Fluttering Blade on that weapon.

Overcharge: You may spend 1 RP or 1 mana to make it assist an ally within 30ft of the sword instead. 

Requirements

Heavy Spin Fluttering Blade Active. You or an ally is the target of an attack and your chosen Heavy Spin Fluttering Blade is not part of that attack.

Ability

Fae Knight Style: Megasoma

0 RP
Range30ft
Keywords
Rapid

You direct a Heavy Spin Fluttering Blade at a target within 30ft of you, making a Heavy attack and cancelling Fluttering Blade on that weapon afterwards.

The chosen Blade must have Line of Sight to the chosen target.

Requirements

Can only be used with Heavy Spin Fluttering Blades that have been active for at least 1 whole round.

Ability

Fae Knight Style: Gadfly

2 AP
Range90ft
Keywords
Challenge

Sends a Fluttering Blade to harass the target within 90ft of you, making a light attack against them. 

The Fluttering Blade then occupies the same space as the target and follows them throughout all movement, including teleportation. 

The Gadfly then Challenges the target. You can upgrade this attack of opportunity to a heavy attack for 1 RP or 1 Mana.

Requirements

Fluttering Blade Active

4 AP2 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Secret ArtIllusionActive

Creates 5 Illusionary Heavy Spin Fluttering Blades within 20ft from you that last until the end of the encounter.
