Key ability

Enduring Earth

Class feature

Benefit 1

You may spend your Encounter Start to enter the Steadfast Earth Stance for 0 mana.

Benefit 2

After entering Steadfast Earth Stance, you keep the benefit until the end of the encounter even if you leave the stance afterwards. After doing so, you are considered to be in Earth Stance for all abilities that require it.

Benefit 3

After using Hardening Fist, you may choose to let the effect last until the end of the encounter. You can still remove the effect for 0 AP.

Seismic Reversal

0 RP2 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Circuit

You grab the target’s weapon or arm and pull it anywhere adjacent to you before slamming the target to the ground and knocking the target Prone.

Does not work against targets more than 1 size larger than you.

If a target defies your Mandate and is within 10ft of you, you may use this on them after their attack is resolved.

Requirements
