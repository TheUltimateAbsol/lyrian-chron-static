Improvised Weaponry

Class feature

Benefit 1

When using improvised weapons, they deal the same damage as normal weapons would.

Benefit 2

You may use Chair Grab.

Benefit 3

Upon mastering this class, your improvised weapon attacks all gain the Prone keyword.
