Key ability

Dark Weapon

Class feature

Benefit 1

When attacking with a weapon, you may choose to have the weapon deal Dark Damage instead of its normal damage type. You must declare your chosen damage type before making the accuracy roll.

Ability

Lone Wolf Stance

No listed cost
RangeSelf
Keywords
Encounter StartStance

You enter a stance in which you declare you fight alone. This lasts until the end of the encounter or until you are Downed.

While in this stance, you cannot become the target of allied abilities, except AoE ones that also hit enemies at the same time, nor any other abilities that buff allies such as aura's. At the same time, you cannot use abilities on allies either except damaging ones that also work on enemies (including aura type abilities again). Abilities or effects that require a willing ally or you to be a willing ally for someone also do not work.

However, you may use Aid abilities on yourself.
