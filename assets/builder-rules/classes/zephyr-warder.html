Glide

Wind Infusion

1 AP0 MP
Range30ft
Keywords
SpellTypeWind

You surround the willing target in protective winds, changing their typing to Wind II. As a Wind II type, they take regular damage from all other elements, but only half damage from Wind or Earth damage or from attacks with the Wind or Earth keyword unless that attack deals true damage. They take 50% more damage from Frost damage or attacks with the Frost keyword. While the ward lasts, they gain a +2 bonus to their Evasion against ranged weapon attacks. The ward lasts until the end of the encounter or until their type is overwritten by a more powerful type.

Ability

Wind Wall

2 AP1 MP
Range60ft
Keywords
ConcentrationSpellWind

You create a wall of wind that is 30ft long, 5ft wide and 10ft high that slows down all ranged weapon attacks passing through it, treating them as having only half the normal range. This wall must be a straight line. These attacks also lose the Lock On, Sure Hit, Half Pierce, Guard Crush and Full Pierce keywords. In addition, the targets of those attacks are treated as having +2 Evasion and +1 Guard. Any Spells that are considered attacks (those that require an accuracy roll against a person that isn’t using the Block reaction) that pass through the wall also suffer this penalty.
