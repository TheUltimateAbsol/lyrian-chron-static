Maximum Firerate

4 AP3 MP
RangeRanged Weapon Range
Keywords
Salvo

You make 8 attacks against the target. You roll these salvo attacks in pairs of two rather than all at the same time. Any reactions from an enemy are still valid against all 8 like a normal salvo.

After every 2 attacks, you may choose to cancel further attacks. You regain 1 mana spent on this ability for every 2 shots you did not fire due to cancelling.

Requirements

Must be made with a pistol, musket or crossbow.

Ability

Suppressive Fire

3 AP1 MP
RangeRanged Weapon Range
Keywords
Salvo

You make 5 light attacks divided against enemy targets in a 25x25ft area.

Afterwards, all enemies that you damaged are treated as blinded 2 when making ranged attacks and you may make a ranged weapon light attack against them each time they do. If they walk out of the 25x25 area, they are no longer affected by this.

These effects lasts until the start of your next turn.

Requirements

Must be made with a pistol, musket or crossbow.
