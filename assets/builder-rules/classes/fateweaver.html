Thread of Reprieve

1 AP
Granted byThread of Reprieve
Range60ft
Keywords
Active

You apply the Threads of Reprieve to a single ally until the start of your next turn 

If that ally takes damage while the threads remain attached, you may spend 1 mana to heal them 3+Focus, up to the damage they took.

Ability

Thread of Defiance

1 AP
Range60ft
Keywords
Active

You apply the Threads of Defiance to a single ally until the start of your next turn that grants them a +2 bonus to saves.

If that ally fails a Save while the threads are attached, you may spend 1 Mana to grant them a bonus to that save equal to your Focus.

Ability

Thread of Deliverance

1 AP
Range60ft
Keywords
ActiveAid

You apply the Threads of Deliverance to a single ally until the start of your next turn that increases their Dodge by 2 and Block by 4. 

While the threads remain attached, the first time an ally takes the Dodge action, but fails to avoid the attack or takes the Block action but fails to fully block the attack, you may spend 2 mana to make them regain 1 RP.

Ability

Thread of Triumph

1 AP
Range60ft
Keywords
ActiveAid

You apply the Threads of Triumph to a single ally to reduce their critical hit range by 1 (IE: you crit on an 19-20 instead of a 20) until they score a critical hit.

If the target scores a critical hit while the threads remain attached, you may spend 1 RP to give them haste.

Ability

Atropos ~ Sever the Thread

1 RP2 MP
Range60ft

You cut the threads of fate you attached to an ally, removing a single Fateweaver thread from them and granting them an effect.

The ally gains an effect based on what thread was cut:

Reprieve: The target is healed for 6+2 x your Focus

Defiance: The target gains a bonus to their next save equal to your Focus.

Deliverance: The target regains 1 RP.

Triumph: The target gains Haste

Requirements

An ally within range has one of your threads attached to them.
