Key ability

Authority

Class feature

Benefit 1

You may treat any roll on Negotiation and Intimidation checks that are below 10 as being a 10 and may spend 2 mana before rolling to treat the roll as a 20 instead.

Benefit 2

One of your eyes becomes an Domination Eye. It may be used to make Mystic Eye type abilities of the appropriate type. An eye can only be a single mystic eye type.
