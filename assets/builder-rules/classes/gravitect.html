Key ability

Gravity Master

Class feature

Benefit 1

When you push or pull an enemy on your turn, you may spend 1 mana to increase this distance by 10ft. You may also spend 1 mana to instead ignore the effects of 1 source of Immovable on a target you’re attempting to push, unless that target is permanently Immovable.

Singularity Collapse

1 AP1 MP
Range60ft
Keywords
SpellGravity

You target a 25x25 square and all creatures within that square must make a save or be pulled 10ft towards the center.

Ability

Weight of the World

1 RP
Range40ft
Keywords
Gravity

You increase the target’s gravity, reducing their elevation by 40ft. This may interrupt movement and if you reduce their elevation down to 0, you slam them into the ground dealing fall damage for the distance they failed to move and knocking them prone.

Requirements

Target enemy is flying or jumping.

Secret Art

Secret Art: Black Hole

2 AP2 MP
Range60ft
Keywords
SpellGravitySecret Art

You create a Black Hole at a target location within range. When you cast this and when you pay upkeep, all enemies within 120ft of the black hole must make a save or get pulled 20ft towards it.

All enemies within 120ft of the black hole that are moving away from it are considered slowed for that movement.

Upkeep: 1 Mana. Instead of paying upkeep, you may detonate the Black Hole making a Heavy attack against all enemies within 15ft of the black hole.
