Key ability

Mass Index

Class feature

Benefit 1

Your spells with the Gravity keyword deal extra damage to the target based on its size. They deal an additional 1 damage to large creatures and an additional 3 to Huge creatures.

Benefit 2

You may use Gravity Crush.
