Key ability

Mime's Journey

Class feature
Grants+5 skill pointsArt

Benefit 1

You gain proficiency in 1 common and 1 specialized weapon

Benefit 2

You gain +5 skill points to spend in Art. You can exchange any skill point for 2 expertise points, but must spend them in this skill.

Benefit 3

As a mime, you’re not allowed to speak. If you speak during an encounter, you cannot use any Mime abilities until the end of the encounter.

Ability

Imaginary Weapon

0 AP
RangeSelf
Keywords
QuickIllusion

You ‘draw’ an imaginary weapon or shield in which you are proficient. 

This weapon works exactly like a normal weapon would, but only you can wield it and normal people can't see it.

Ability

Invisible Wall

1 AP
Range5ft
Keywords
Illusion

You create an invisible 5ft wide and high wall within range. It gives Low Cover, but it counts as High Cover against the first ranged attacks the wall gives cover from.

Ability

Dramatic Death

1 RP
RangeSelf

You fall prone and may immediately use the Play Dead action. You may use your Art(Mime) for this roll instead of Deception.

At the start of your turn, you may stop playing dead and immediately stand up from prone for 0 AP.

Requirements

You've taken damage and that damage is done resolving.

Ability

Imaginary Consumables

1 APVariable MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Illusion

You ‘draw’ an imaginary basic consumable alchemy item or minor item that can cost no more than 100c per mana spent on this ability and no more than 500c total. 

This consumable works like regular consumables would, but only you can use it, normal people can’t see it and it doesn’t work with abilities that require you to use real consumables like counter-potion.

Any items created with this ability only last until the end of the encounter, or for 5 minutes outside of one.
