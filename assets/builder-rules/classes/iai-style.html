Key ability

Iaijutsu

Class feature

Benefit 1

If you become the target of an attack while you're surprised, you may still react to it and any special properties of an attack that require you to be unaware of the attack (such as Killshot) do not apply to it.

Benefit 2

You may sheathe and draw katanas for 0 AP once per turn per katana.

Benefit 3

You start each battle in the 'Iai' state, which is used for Iai abilities. Making an attack with a katana removes the Iai state for that weapon. Using a skill that requires the Iai state requires the weapon to be sheathed and will remove the Iai state for ALL your weapons, unless specified otherwise.

Ability

Uke Nagashi

1 RP
RangeMelee Weapon Range
Keywords
CounterIaijutsu

Roll Heavy Damage and treat that as your Block against the attack that’s targeting you. If you reduce the damage to 0, the attacker becomes Unbalanced. On hit effects and maces do not gain their effect if you reduce the damage to 0. After the attack is resolved, you may immediately make an Iai Strike against the attacker for 0 RP. This Iai Strike gains the Sure Hit keyword against an Unbalanced target. 

Requirements

Katana only, target of a melee attack and must be in the Iai state.
