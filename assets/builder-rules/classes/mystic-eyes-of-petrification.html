Key ability

Petrification Eye

Class feature

Benefit 1

One of your eyes becomes a Petrification Eye. It may be used to make Mystic Eye type abilities of the appropriate type. An eye can only be a single mystic eye type.

Benefit 2

You may use Unpetrify.
