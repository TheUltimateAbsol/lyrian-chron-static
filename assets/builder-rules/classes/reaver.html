Overwhelming Critical

Class feature

Benefit 1

When you roll a critical hit while making a Heavy attack with a Two-Handed weapon you may use Aftershock even if the attack didn’t miss. This use doesn’t count against the normal use limit of it and this effect still applies even if the critical hit was blocked or otherwise defended against.

Ability

Sweeping Stance

2 AP1 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Stance

You enter a stance in which you spin your weapon around in a circular motion and lash out against any enemy that approaches. When an enemy enters your weapon’s range you may make an Attack of Opportunity against them. An enemy moving while already being in your attack range does not provoke. Your weapon's range for these attacks of opportunities is increased by 5ft (This does not affect your normal threaten range).

The stance lasts until you make an attack that isn’t an Attack of Opportunity.

Requirements

Two-handed weapons only.

Secret Art

Secret Art: Bladestorm

2 AP4 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Secret ArtStanceUpkeepScatter

You enter a stance in which you continuously spin your weapon around your body. When activating this ability, you may make a Heavy attack against all enemies within your melee weapon range. While the stance lasts you may make a Heavy attack against all enemies within your melee weapon range at the start of your turn.

You can move while in this stance. If you move close enough to an enemy for them to be within your melee weapon range, you may make a Heavy attack against them.

You can only attack a creature once per round through this ability, regardless of how many times you move closer to them.

Upkeep: 2 Mana, 1 AP

Requirements

Two-handed weapons only. Slashing weapons only.
