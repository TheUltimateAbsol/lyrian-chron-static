Key ability

Fire Weapon

Class feature

Benefit 1

When attacking with a weapon, you may choose to have the weapon deal Fire Damage instead of its normal damage type. You must declare your chosen damage type before making the accuracy roll.

Ability

Trial By Fire

1 AP
RangeMelee Weapon Range
Keywords
Challenge

You make a Light attack against the target. On damage, they are challenged by you until the start of your next turn.

 If this does not deal damage, they cannot be challenged by you until the start of your next turn.

Ability

Scorching Reprisal

1 RP
RangeMelee Weapon Range
Keywords
Opportunistic

You make a Heavy attack of opportunity against the target. Your next basic attack of opportunity against that target before the start of your next turn costs 0 RP.

Requirements

An enemy provokes an attack of opportunity.

Ability

Molten Domain

1 AP
RangeSelf
Keywords
DomainBurning

Until the start of your next turn, the ground 5ft around you becomes like burning ground.

Any enemy willingly leaving or moving through this area other than entering it becomes Burning X, where X is your Focus x 3.

The domain burns away other friendly damage aura effects while in effect. Effects that would normally trigger upon ending the turn in it (such as Poison Spore) don't trigger if someone ends their turn in the Molten Domain.

Ability

Infernal Bastion

1 RP1 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Burning

You surround yourself with flames and take the Block action against the attack. If you reduce damage of this attack to 0, inflict Burning X on the target where X is your focus x 3.

Requirements

You are targeted by a melee attack.
