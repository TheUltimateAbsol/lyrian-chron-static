Key ability

Rock and Stone

Class feature

Benefit 1

You gain proficiency in 1 armor or in the shield. You also gain proficiency in the Pickaxe specialty weapon.

Benefit 2

You gain the ability to gather mining outcrops.

Benefit 3

You gain the ability to create Ingots as an Errand. See item: Blacksmithing Materials. You may use an IP to do this 4 times instead.

Ability

Power Rock Strike

1 Strike Dice Other

Make a gathering check at only half of your gathering bonus (rounded down) add the rolled amount to both node and lucky points.

Requirements

Must in a gathering session. 

Ability

Excavation Crash

1 AP1 MP
RangeMelee Weapon Range

Choose one adjacent location 5ft from the target. The target may immediately use a move action for 0 RP to move there if they wish, you cannot make an attack of opportunity during Excavation Crash. If the target does not move to the location, make a heavy attack against them.

Ability

Efficient Strike

1 Strike Dice Other

Make a gathering check and apply it to NP. If this maxes node points, all overflow is converted into lucky points.

Requirements

Must in a gathering session. 

Ability

Rock Fang

3 AP
RangeMelee Weapon Range
Keywords
Sunder

Make a heavy attack against the target. On damage the target is affected by Armor Sunder 2, reducing their guard by 2 until the start of your next turn. This attack also immediately benefits from the Sunder effect.
