Key ability

Natural Cycle

Class feature

Benefit 1

At the start of your turn you may pay upkeep 1 mana for each Regrowth you have on an ally. If you do, you may extend its duration by 1 turn. You can choose this upkeep separately for each Regrowth.

Benefit 2

You may use Regrowth.
