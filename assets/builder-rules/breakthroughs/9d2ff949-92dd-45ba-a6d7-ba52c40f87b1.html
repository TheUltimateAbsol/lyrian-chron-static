Summon Contract: Ixilune, The Swarm

0 EXP

Appearance: Hot bug lady.
Desires: Ixilune desires to consume everything.
Pact Cost: You need to eat 1.5x as much food to be fed.
Attunement Cost: None
Re-Summon Cost: Must eat the flesh of a creature of the same (main) race.

When you contract Ixilune, you are able to attune to it. While attuned, you gain access the Distracting Swarm ability through your Channel Eidolon ability. You summon the Devouring Swarm for your Lesser Eidolon and Ixilune for your Eidolon. (The stats for these are listed under monsters.)

Requirements

Must be a summoner and have visited the eidolon.

Unlocked ability

Channel Eidolon: Distracting Swarm

1 AP1 MP
Range60ft

You summon a distracting swarm around a target. The target immediately loses any Concentration effects and suffers -5 penalty to Perception checks unless they spend 1 RP to sidestep out of the effect. If they did not move, they also cannot use any abilities with the Concentration keyword until they move.

You can only have 1 Distracting Swarm active at the same time.

Requirements

Ixilune attunement.
