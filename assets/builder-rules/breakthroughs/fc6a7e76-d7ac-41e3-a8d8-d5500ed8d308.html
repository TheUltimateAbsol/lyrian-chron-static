Breakthrough

Divine Luck II

600 EXP

Once per arc, you may choose to automatically succeed on a check (if success is possible). 

This may be used after a roll is made for a check. 

If used for an accuracy check, it sets your accuracy equal to the minimum value required to pass at the time of its use. This cannot give you a value higher than what is possible for you to achieve.

Requirements

Must have purchased Divine Luck I.
