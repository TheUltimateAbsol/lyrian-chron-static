Ability

Anti-Magic Shield

1 RP
Range60ft
Keywords
AidArcaneShieldSpell

You grant the target ally 1 RP that must be spend on a basic Dodge or Block action against the triggering attack as well as Anti-Magic Shield for the triggering attack. Anti-Magic Shield reduces the damage the target ally takes by your Focus.

Requirements

Ally is the target of a damage dealing Spell.
