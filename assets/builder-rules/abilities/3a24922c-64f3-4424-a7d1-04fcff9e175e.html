Ability

Parry

1 RP1 MP
Range-
Keywords
Overcharge

Roll your Weapon Damage, but add your Focus instead of Power to the roll and treat that as your Block against the attack targeting you. If the triggering attack is a light attack, you roll light weapon damage. Otherwise, you roll your Heavy damage.

If Parry reduces the damage to 0, you regain 1 RP (cannot go higher than your max RP). On hit effects and maces do not gain their effect if you reduce the damage to 0.

When using Parry against an attack, that attack cannot land a critical hit against you.

Overcharge: You may spend 1 additional mana to roll Heavy damage even if the triggering attack is a light attack.

Requirements

Target of a melee attack, wielding a One-Handed melee weapon.
