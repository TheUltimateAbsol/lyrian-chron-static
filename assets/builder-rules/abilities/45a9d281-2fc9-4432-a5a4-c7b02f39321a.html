Ability

Spellblade's Preparation

No listed cost
RangeSelf
Keywords
EnchantmentEncounter Start

You unsheathe a melee weapon and enchant that weapon with an element you've mastered. Your weapon now deals the chosen element’s damage instead of its normal damage.

If you are not holding the enchanted weapon at the start of your turn, your weapon loses this benefit.

The weapon gains a +1 Power bonus to attacks until the end of the encounter.
