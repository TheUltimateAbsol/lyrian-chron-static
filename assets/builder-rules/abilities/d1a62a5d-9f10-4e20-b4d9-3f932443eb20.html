Ability

Soul Strike

0 AP
Range60ft
Keywords
Killing BlowOvercharge

After landing a killing blow, you can use Soul Strike once on that target.  If they are still at 0 HP or less, you raise an orb of divine energy out of the target, killing them instantly.  You then use that orb against a second target, making a light attack that deals dark damage.  It is possible to use Soul Strike while in Cat Form. Overcharge: You may spend 1 extra AP to instead make a heavy or precise attack.

Requirements

You land a killing blow.
