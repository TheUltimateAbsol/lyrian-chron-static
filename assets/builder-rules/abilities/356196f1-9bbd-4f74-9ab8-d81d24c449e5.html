Ability

Wind Shield

1 RP
Range30ft
Keywords
AidShieldSpell

You grant the target  1 RP that must be spent on the basic Dodge or Block action against the triggering attack as well as Wind Shield. Wind Shield grants a bonus to Dodge equal to your Focus against the triggering attack. The triggering attack can also no longer benefit from Lock On or Sure Hit

Requirements

Ally is targeted by a ranged attack.
