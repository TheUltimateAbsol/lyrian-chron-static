Ability

No getting past!

0 RP
RangeMelee Weapon Range
Keywords
Sure HitOpportunistic

You may make a Light (0 RP) or Heavy (1 RP) attack against the target. On damage, the target becomes Slowed until the end of their turn. If the target is already Slowed, they become Rooted.

Requirements

Only usable when you would be able to make an attack of opportunity.
