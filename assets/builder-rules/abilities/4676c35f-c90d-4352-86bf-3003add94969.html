Ability

Rage Stance

2 AP2 MP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Stance

You enter a Stance in which you prioritize offense over defense. The Stance lasts until the encounter ends, you get Downed or up to an hour. While the Stance lasts, gain 2 additional AP that can only be used on melee attacks each turn (including the turn you use this), but cannot use actions that use RP other than attack of opportunity unless they have the Rage keyword. You also cannot use abilities with the Spell keyword, unless they also have the Rage keyword. You are treated as having 0 RP for abilities with that as a requirement. While in your Rage Stance, you gain a +1 bonus to Guard and a +5 bonus to all Fitness checks.
