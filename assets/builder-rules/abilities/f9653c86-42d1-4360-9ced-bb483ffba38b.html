Ability

Medusa's Glance

1 RP
RangeSelf
Keywords
Calcification

You flare your mystic eye as you are attacked. 

The attacker must avert their eyes and become Blinded 5 until the attack resolves or begin to suffer to partial petrification. They lose 1 RP and gain 1 stack of Calcification.

Requirements

You are the target of a melee attack.
