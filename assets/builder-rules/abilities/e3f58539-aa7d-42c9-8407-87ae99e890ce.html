Ability

Haunting Bark

1 AP
Range60ft
Keywords
Mind AffectingFearShakenFull PierceSound

You bark at an enemy forcing them to make a Save or become shaken until the end of their next turn. The third time in a combat you use this against the same target, they also take Light Full Pierce Lightning damage in addition to the shaken effect if they fail their save.

Requirements

Must be in Dog Form.
