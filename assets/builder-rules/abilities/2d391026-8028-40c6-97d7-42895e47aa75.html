Ability

Aurora Blade Style: Aurora Slash

2 AP1 MP
Range80ft
Keywords
SlowFrost

You fire slashes in a straight line creating a curtain of light which strikes the first target hit with a light attack that deals frost damage. On hit the target is slowed until the end of your next turn. 

The light from the slash continues for the full range, creating a 300ft high wall that blocks vision on both sides. This wall of multicolored light last until the end of your next turn.

Requirements

Light swords, longsword, katana or dueling weapon only.
