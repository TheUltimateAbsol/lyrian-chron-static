Salvo

You make 8 attacks against the target. You roll these salvo attacks in pairs of two rather than all at the same time. Any reactions from an enemy are still valid against all 8 like a normal salvo.

After every 2 attacks, you may choose to cancel further attacks. You regain 1 mana spent on this ability for every 2 shots you did not fire due to cancelling.

Requirements

Must be made with a pistol, musket or crossbow.
