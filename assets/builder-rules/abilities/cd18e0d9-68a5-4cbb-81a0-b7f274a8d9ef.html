Ability

White Thunder

1 AP
Range80ft
Keywords
FrostSlowSpellScatter

You target a Glacial Wall in range and fire a bolt of Frost energy that requires line of sight. On impact, the wall is destroyed and explodes into blades of ice, attacking all enemies within 5ft of the destroyed wall with a Precise attack that deals Frost damage. On damage, the target is Slowed until the end of their next turn.
