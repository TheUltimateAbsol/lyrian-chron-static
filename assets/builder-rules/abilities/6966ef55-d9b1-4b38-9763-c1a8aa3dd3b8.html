Ability

Corpse Domination

3 AP4 MP
Range30ft
Keywords
Summon

You raise the target under your command. A target raised in this way is treated as a non-heroic unit that takes its own actions, however it will try to act in a way that is most helpful to you in the fight. The raised target keeps all of its stats and abilities it had in life, though it only starts with half its maximum health (before any reductions) and whatever mana it had left over when it died. This summon lasts until the end of the encounter or until its HP reaches 0, upon which it immediately dies again and cannot be raised again in any way.

Requirements

Target must be a dead humanoid.
