Ability

Thread of Deliverance

1 AP
Range60ft
Keywords
ActiveAid

You apply the Threads of Deliverance to a single ally until the start of your next turn that increases their Dodge by 2 and Block by 4. 

While the threads remain attached, the first time an ally takes the Dodge action, but fails to avoid the attack or takes the Block action but fails to fully block the attack, you may spend 2 mana to make them regain 1 RP.
