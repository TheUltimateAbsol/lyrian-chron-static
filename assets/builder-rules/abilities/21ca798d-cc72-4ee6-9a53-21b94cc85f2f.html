Ability

Trail Mix

Rest Action Other

For a camp action you create up to 5 servings of a trail meal.  It costs 20u of food per serving and only gives 1 mana (0 for Mirane), but it keeps starvation away.  Does not require a culinary kit, and does not count as cooking for the purposes of culinary actions such as using stocks.

Requirements

20u of Food Per Serving
