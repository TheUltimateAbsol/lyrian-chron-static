Ability

Wind-Empowered Aim

2 AP
RangeSelf
Keywords
AuraUpkeep

You project Wind-Empowered Aim aura around yourself that empowers the ranged attacks of your allies. When a heroic ally within 20ft of you (including yourself) makes a ranged attack, they may add 1d4 to the accuracy roll. Upkeep: You must spend 1 AP at the start of each turn or the Aura fades.
