Maid Training

Class feature
Grants+5 skill pointsAppraise, Athletics, Common Knowledge, History, Insight, Intimidation, Linguistics, Medicine, Perception, Roguecraft, Stealth, Survival

Benefit 1

You gain proficiency in staves (broom), small weapons, sets of missiles and light armor.

Benefit 2

You gain 5 skill points to spend in Athletics, Stealth, Roguecraft, Medicine, Common Knowledge, Linguistics, Appraise, History, Perception, Insight, Survival or Intimidation. You can exchange any skill point for 2 expertise points, but must spend them in these skills.

Benefit 3

You may use Graceful Service.
