Key ability

Rock and Stone

Class feature

Benefit 1

You gain proficiency in 1 armor or in the shield. You also gain proficiency in the Pickaxe specialty weapon.

Benefit 2

You gain the ability to gather mining outcrops.

Benefit 3

You gain the ability to create Ingots as an Errand. See item: Blacksmithing Materials. You may use an IP to do this 4 times instead.
