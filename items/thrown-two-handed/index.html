Weapon effect

Switch Hit

This weapon can be used as a melee weapon without penalty. After making a ranged attack with this weapon, it returns to your hand.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Skirmisher (35 points):
    After making a ranged attack with this weapon, you may move 5ft as if you had the light sword passive (Nimble).
  • Spinning Axe (35 Points):
    This weapon also gains the axe passive (Wild Swing). You keep this effect even when throwing the weapon.

B mods

  • Throwing Hammer (50 points):
    Attacks with this weapon gain the Artillery keyword within 30ft.
  • Ricocheting (50 points):
    Once per round when this weapon deals damage with a Heavy attack, you may make a Light attack against a different target within 20ft.
