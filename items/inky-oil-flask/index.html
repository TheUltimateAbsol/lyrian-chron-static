Alchemy · Flask

Inky Oil Flask

Item details

Cost500 Clim
Burden1
Range40ft
Activation
1 AP
Crafting points
50
Crafting discipline
Alchemy

An alchemical mix aimed at the target's eyes. May be thrown 40ft away. When thrown, the target of the flask takes 1 damage. If they took damage, they are Blinded 2 until they spend 2 AP to wipe the substance off.

Crafting requirements

  • Materials: 1000 Alchemy units
  • 1x Rare Water

Static edition of Lyrian Chronicles 0.13.1. Content and artwork sourced from Angel’s Sword Studios.
