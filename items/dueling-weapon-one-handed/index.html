Dueling Weapon (One-Handed)

Item details

Cost500 Clim
Burden1
Range5ft
Crafting points
30
Crafting discipline
Blacksmithing

Weapon effect

Dueling

When attacking a single enemy during a turn at least 3 times with this weapon, your next Dodge or Block against that enemy costs 0 RP until the start of your next turn. This property cannot be used if you target any other enemy during your turn or if you spend AP making a Heavy attack against the target.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Spade Lunga (10 points):
    When moving at least 40ft towards an enemy, increases the range of your next attack made with this weapon by 5 ft.
  • Ornate Blade (35 points):
    Moving into the target’s threatened zone the first time in a turn counts as 1 attack for the requirement to activate Dueling.

B mods

  • Paired Duelers (25 points):
    When wielding two dueling weapons with paired duelers, your offhand extra attack counts as one attack for your dueling bonus on your main hand weapon.
  • Passato Sotto (80 points):
    You may make 1 Heavy attack with this weapon and still gain the dueling effect.
  • Ferrobri Grip (80 points):
     Dueling weapon passive changes to Aggressive Dueling: When attacking a single enemy 3 times with this weapon in 1 turn, your next basic attack with this weapon this turn has its cost reduced by 1 AP. Once per round.
