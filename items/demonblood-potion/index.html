Demonblood Potion

Item details

Cost500 Clim
Burden1
Activation
0 AP
Crafting points
50
Crafting discipline
Alchemy

Not actually made out of Demonblood, it refers to Demon tenacity.  The potion is an intense stimulant that allows a character to fight through pain and injury. When drank, you ignore the penalties of one injury until the end of the encounter.

Crafting requirements

  • Materials: 250 Alchemy units
  • 1x Rare Arcane
  • 1x Rare Fire

Static edition of Lyrian Chronicles 0.13.1. Content and artwork sourced from Angel’s Sword Studios.
