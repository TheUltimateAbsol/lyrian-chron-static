Equipment · Armor

Armor (Light)

Item details

Cost500 Clim
Burden1
Crafting points
30
Crafting discipline
Armorsmithing

Increases your Guard by 1 and your Block by 4, but reduces your Initiative by 1 and your Evasion and Dodge by 2.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Agile Weave (35 points):
    No armor penalty, but no guard/block bonus. You gain +1 evasion (but no additional dodge). This also takes up your Frame mod slot.
  • Acrobat's Harness (10 points):
    You gain +10 bonus to athletics while wearing this armor.
  • Camo (10 points):
    Pick a color theme. You gain +5 to stealth when hiding in environments of that theme, but -5 when hiding elsewhere. A GM may approve new environments, defaults are: green camo (jungle, woodland, prone in grass), yellow camo (desert, savannas), white camo (snow, arctic), dark camo (night, caves, rocky terrain), blue camo (rivers or oceanic)
  • Porcupine Hide (10 points):
    Enemies grappling you take 1d6 full pierce damage. When escaping a grapple, you also deal 1d6 full pierce damage to the creature grappling you.

B mods

  • Sleek (25 points):
    If you spend RP dodging an attack of opportunity, you may dodge any other attacks of opportunity made in reaction to the same action at the first for free.
  • Padded (25 points):
    Increase guard vs light attacks by 1, doesn't affect heavy damage.
  • Mistweave (50 points):
    You no longer provoke attacks of opportunity, but all attacks against you gain Half Pierce.
