The Sparrow Model Aerial Staff design is the most simple entry level Aerial Staff.  It has a base speed of 20 feet and does not include integrated shielding or any weapon systems.  Often used for training, its fuel efficiency is fairly high. 

Its user can use it to fly. It has a speed of a 20ft.

Requires 1 hand to operate while flying.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Jury-Rigged Cannon (10 points):
    Adds a small star cannon that fires an arcane blast from the front that deals heavy attack damage for 2 AP with 120ft Range. 
  • Medivac Rig (20 points):
    Allows a downed ally to be hauled on the underbelly of the staff. But reduces the speed by 10ft while hauling. 
  • Jury Rigged Shield (35 points):
    Adds a shield generator, granting the airship a 15 HP shield. This shield can be switched on and off, but can only be replenished by changing the fuel cell. 

B mods

  • Sorthen Shield Generator (50 points):
    Requires Jury-Rigged Shield. Installs a Sorthen style Shield Generator increasing the shield value by 15 HP.
  • Upgraded Airship Engine (80 points):
    Increases the speed to 50ft but adds Line Movement: The airship may only move in a straight line when making a movement action.
