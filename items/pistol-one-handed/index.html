Weapon effect

Two-Handed Grip

You may sheath or draw this weapon for 0 AP once per turn. When you use two hands to make attacks with this weapon, you get a +1 (Light or Heavy attack) or +2 (Precise attack) bonus to your accuracy roll.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Blast Diverter (10 points):
    Once per turn, attacks from this weapon cause the target to only be able to make heavy attacks of opportunity  against you until the end of your turn.
  • Double Action (35 points):
    Accuracy is reduced by 3, but attacks with this weapon reduce the target’s ranged accuracy by 1 (stacking) until the start of your next turn regardless of if they hit or not.  Doesn’t affect boss enemies.

B mods

  • Match Barrel (50 points):
    The range of this weapon is increased by 60ft.
  • Beavertail Grip (50 points):
    When making a light attack at a target you can move 10ft in a straight line towards them.
