Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Mastercrafted Saya (20 points):
    The first single target attack made with this weapon per combat also draws the weapon simultaneously.  This first attack has its crit threshold lowered by 1.
  • Sori Curve (20 points):
    When you roll a 1 on an accuracy roll, you can reroll the attack. Your target(s) can make an opportunity attack, even with a ranged weapon.  This opportunity attack does not provoke other opportunity attacks.
  • Beneri Special (35 points):
    Grants a +1/+1 Bonus, doesn’t stack with material bonuses. Weapon shatters, becoming irreparably broken on a 1 roll for an accuracy roll.

B mods

  • Equilibrium Forging (25 points):
    You do not roll damage on this weapon, but deal slightly higher than average damage. Instead it deals 6 damage on light attacks or 15 damage (1h) or 19 damage (2h). Critical strikes apply maximum damage as normal.
  • Hamon Line (50 points):
    Grants Keen II lowers the crit threshold by 2 (replaces Keen).
  • Swallow's Edge (80 points):
    The crit threshold decreases by 1 for each attack made this turn with a Swallow’s Edge weapon. This bonus resets if you land a critical hit. If both your mainhand and offhand weapon have this mod, the effect aplies to both weapons at the same time.
