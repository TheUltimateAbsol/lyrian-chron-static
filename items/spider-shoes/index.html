A pair of shoes that can be adjusted to fit the wearer. Upon use, the wearer is able to walk across any angled surface as if it was normal solid ground

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Web Resistance (10 points):
    You don't suffer any penalties from walking across webs either natural or created through magic or items.
  • Quick Activation (20 points):
    The activation cost is reduced by 1 AP, to a minimum of 0.
  • Ceiling Drop (20 points):
    You may cancel the effect of this artifice for 0 RP. When you do, if you were attached to a ceiling or vertical surface, treat the fall as if it was 100ft lower than it is.

B mods

  • Sticky Footing (50 points):
    You gain a +5 bonus to any saves that would knock you prone or knock you back.
  • Skitter Away (80 points):
    When you move up a vertical surface from a horizontal one, your first movement of the round does not provoke an attack of opportunity.
