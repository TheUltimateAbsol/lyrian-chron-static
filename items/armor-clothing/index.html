Equipment · Armor

Armor (Clothing)

Item details

Cost500 Clim
Burden0
Crafting points
30
Crafting discipline
Armorsmithing

A high quality set of clothing. Does not provide any bonuses by itself, but if crafted by an armorsmith, can accept Flamellon mods.

Cannot be worn at the same time as regular armor.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Mage Robes (20 points):
    You gain +1 maximum mana, but lose 1 guard and 2 block.

B mods

  • Cloud Robes (50 points):
    Your movement speed is increased by 5ft.
  • Battle Casting (80 points):
    The first spell you cast each turn does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

Frame mods

  • Reinforced Clothing (0 points):
    This clothing can benefit from material bonuses, but its burden is increased by 1.
