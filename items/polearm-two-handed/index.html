Weapon effect

Polearm

You gain +5ft range on your weapon attacks and your threatened range becomes 5-10ft.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Heavy Broadhead (10 points):
    Increases thrown power by 2 but boomerang/returning effects no longer work.
  • Shepherd's Lanza (20 points):
    Fall damage is reduced by 60ft while wielding.
  • Halberd (35 points):
    This weapon also gains the axe passive (Wild Swing).

B mods

  • Winged Lugs (25 points):
    Increase guard by 1 and Block by 2 and reduce evasion by 1 while wielding.
  • Fullpike (25 points):
    Polearm weapon passive changes to +10ft and your threatened range becomes 5-15 ft. Having this item in your burden reduces your max RP by 1.
  • Yari Point Taper (50 points):
    After making a basic heavy attack, strike another target immediately behind the first with a light attack.
  • Swordbreaker (80 points):
     You gain a +2 Swordbreaker Power bonus against targets wielding light blades and heavy blades.
