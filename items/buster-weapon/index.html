Artificing

An artifice that adds an explosive blast to increase forward velocity. The most common type of weapon artifice used to help overwhelm an opponent. Attachable to melee weapons. 

Use when you are making a melee attack against an enemy. The attack gains +3 accuracy and +3 damage.

Can be attached for 4 AP, but once attached, can only be removed by spending an EP in town.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Powerful Explosion (10 points):
    Increases the damage bonus by +1
  • Optimized Explosion (20 points):
    Increases the accuracy bonus by +1

B mods

  • Angel Type (25 points):
    When an attack enhanced by this artifice deals damage, knock the target back 5ft.
  • Launch Step (50 points):
    You can use this before making an attack to move 10ft before making an attack that benefits from this artifice.
  • Shieldbreaker (80 points):
    The attack enhanced by the artifice also gains the Shieldbreaker keyword.
