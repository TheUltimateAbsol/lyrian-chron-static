A small box that comes with two earplugs. It can connect to other devices within a mile by inputting a code unique to the box its connecting to. When a person speaks into the box, their voice can be heard through the earplugs as normal volume, regardless of how loud the person speaks. Only a person wearing at least 1 earplugs can hear sounds coming through it.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Extra Earplugs (10 points):
    The device now comes with 3 additional earplugs, letting more people listen in.

B mods

  • Extra Range (25 points):
    The range of the device increases from 1 mile to 3 miles.
  • Hidden Device (80 points):
    The device is disguises as a regular object, making it not appear as a private communications device. (Closer inspection or perception checks may still reveal it as such).
