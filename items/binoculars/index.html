Artifice · Basic

Binoculars

Item details

Cost500
Burden1
Crafting points
30
Crafting discipline
Artificing

Binoculars let you zoom in on distant views, bringing faraway details up close and clear. 

When using these, you gain a +10 bonus on perception checks against things further than 60ft away but a -10 penalty to things within 60ft.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Quick Focus Dial (20 points):
    The penalty for things within 60ft is reduced from -10 to -5.
  • Night Mode (20 points):
    Looking through the binoculars gives you Nightvision up to 120ft.

B mods

  • Range Finder (25 points):
    When looking through this item, you can tell how far objects are from you.
  • Anti-Illusion Lens (50 points):
    You gain a +10 bonus to determine if something is an Illusion or not.
