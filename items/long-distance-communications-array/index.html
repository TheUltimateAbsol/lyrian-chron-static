A series of boxes, microphones and earplugs that is used for long range communications. It can connect to other devices by inputting a code unique to the artifice its connecting to. Any sound spoken into the microphone is broadcasted to all other artifices connected to it. It has unlimited range.

Static edition of Lyrian Chronicles 0.13.1. Content and artwork sourced from Angel’s Sword Studios.
