Alchemy · Elixir

Ghost Elixir

Item details

Cost500 Clim
Burden1
Range40ft
Activation
0 AP
Crafting points
50
Crafting discipline
Alchemy

A translucent potion that when drunk gives you the phase through trait for 1 minute. This Lets you move through solid objects and creatures. When thrown at a creature with the phase through and/or incorporeal traits, they lose these traits for a minute. Can be thrown up to 40ft.

Crafting requirements

  • Materials: 300 Alchemy units
  • 1x Rare Arcane
  • 1x Rare Wind

Static edition of Lyrian Chronicles 0.13.1. Content and artwork sourced from Angel’s Sword Studios.
