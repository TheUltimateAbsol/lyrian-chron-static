Ghost Elixir

A translucent potion that when drunk gives you the phase through trait for 1 minute. This Lets you move through solid objects and creatures. When thrown at a creature with the phase through and/or incorporeal traits, they lose these traits for a minute. Can be thrown up to 40ft.
