Adventuring Essentials · Basic

Torch

A simple piece of wood with a pre-oiled tied cloth on one end.  Provides ample light and is quite easy to light on fire.  Comes with an attached striker that can be lit on most rock and metal surfaces. When ‘activated’, creates a light that emits light within 30 feet.  Can also be used as an improvised weapon that deals fire damage.  Lasts up to two hours.
