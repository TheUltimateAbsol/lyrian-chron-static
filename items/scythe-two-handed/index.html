Weapon effect

Sweeping Arc

Once per round, when you make a Heavy attack against a target, you may make a Light attack against a different enemy within your range.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Spinning Dance (20 points):
    You may move 5ft after making your Heavy attack, but before making your Sweeping Arc attack.
  • Long Haft (35 points):
    You gain +5ft range on your weapon attacks and your threatened range becomes 10ft.

B mods

  • Rapid Spins (50 points):
    Your Sweeping Arc can now be used two times per round.
  • Focused Spin (50 points):
    Your Sweeping Arc now instead targets the same creature as the target of your Heavy attack.
  • Full Spin (80 points):
    Your sweeping arc can now target all creatures within 5ft of you.
