Marlone Jelly Poison

Item details

Cost400
Burden1
Activation
1 AP
Crafting points
50
Crafting discipline
Alchemy

Contact Poison. Can be applied to a weapon. Lasts for 1 hits or 1 minute (6 rounds).

When an attack with this poison damages a grunt type enemy, the target is stunned until the end of this next turn. An enemy can only be affected by this poison once per combat.

Crafting requirements

  • Materials: 700 Alchemy units
  • 1x Rare Water Herb

Static edition of Lyrian Chronicles 0.13.1. Content and artwork sourced from Angel’s Sword Studios.
