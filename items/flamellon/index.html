Crafting · Materials

Flamellon

A generic term for items attuned to a Flamelic Polarity.

These items are valued at the usual 2 units for 1 clim.

For example, you could have 500 corpse dust that has an Undead Flamelic Polarity. This 500 dust would be valued at 250 clim.

Static edition of Lyrian Chronicles 0.13.1. Content and artwork sourced from Angel’s Sword Studios.
