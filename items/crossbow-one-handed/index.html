Crossbow (One-Handed)

Item details

Cost500 Clim
Burden1
Range60ft
Crafting points
30
Crafting discipline
Carpentry

Weapon effect

Armor Pierce

Treats the enemy's Guard as if it was 1 lower if within 30ft of the target.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Wrist-mounted (35 points):
    One handed crossbow only, range lowered to 30ft. Is mounted on your wrist and is always considered drawn + can hold a weapon in that hand.
  • Windlass Arbalest (35 points):
    Two handed crossbow only. Basic attacks with this weapon gain full pierce within 30 ft. However must be reloaded for 2 AP or 1 RP before it can be used again.

B mods

  • Extended Draw (50 points):
    Your armor piercing effect range is increased to 60ft. If combined with a wrist mounted crossbow, increases the range but not the armor piercing range to 60ft.
  • Repeater (80 points):
    When you make 3 attacks with this weapon in 1 turn, you may make an additional light attack.
