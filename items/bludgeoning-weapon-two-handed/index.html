Equipment · Weapon

Bludgeoning Weapon (Two-Handed)

Item details

Cost1000 Clim
Burden2
Range5ft
Crafting points
30
Crafting discipline
Blacksmithing

Weapon effect

Hollow Blow

When attacking and hitting a target, you deal additional damage if they Block. This damage is 2 on a Light/Precise attack or 4 on a Heavy attack.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Flexible Handle (10 points):
    When dealing damage to a target, the minimum damage you deal is always 2. (this does not trigger on damage effects if damage would be 0 otherwise).
  • Chain Flail (35 points):
    Cannot be defended against by 0 RP actions. Attacks made with this weapon do not count as consuming 0 RP actions.

B mods

  • Weighted Poll Upsetting (25 points):
    When making 3 separate attacks against a target, the third attack you make with “Weighted Poll Upsetting” knocks the target back 10ft on damage.
  • Flanged Head (50 points):
    When you crit with this weapon, the damage is still maximized even against blocking targets.
