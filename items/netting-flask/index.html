Netting Flask

Item details

Cost1200 Clim
Burden1
Range40ft
Activation
1 AP
Crafting points
57
Crafting discipline
Alchemy

A flask that when thrown explodes in a 5ft radius around the target and conjures up magnetic nets. The target and everyone within 5ft of it become Rooted until the start of your next turn or until they use 2 AP to remove the net. May be thrown up to 40ft away

Crafting requirements

  • Materials: 1950 Alchemy units
  • 1x Mystic Wind
  • 1x Rare Earth

Static edition of Lyrian Chronicles 0.13.1. Content and artwork sourced from Angel’s Sword Studios.
