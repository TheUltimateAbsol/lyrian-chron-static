Can be fired while in melee range of an enemy without provoking an attack of opportunity.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Dragon's Breath (10 points):
    The first basic attack you make with this weapon in a round inflicts burning 5 regardless of it hits or not.
  • Proof Load (20 points):
    When you make a heavy attack with this weapon, you may knock yourself back 5ft.
  • Point Blank (20 points):
    You may make AoO’s as if this was a melee weapon with a 5ft threaten range.

B mods

  • Slugs (50 points):
    Your range is doubled.
  • Double Barrel (50 points):
    Two-handed only. The first single target heavy attack you make with this weapon per round also light attacks the same target afterwards. This counts as your dual wield attack.
  • Open Choke (80 points):
    The first basic light attack you make with this weapon in a round makes a Light attack against all enemies in a 45 degree cone up to range.
