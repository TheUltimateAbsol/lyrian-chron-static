Two small boxes that are connected to each other. When a button is pressed and both are active, all audio from one of the boxes is transmitted to the other one. It has a maximum range of 1 mile.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Directional Ping (35 points):
    While within the device's range, you can determine the direction the paired device is in.

B mods

  • Extra Range (25 points):
    The range of the device increases from 1 mile to 2 miles.
