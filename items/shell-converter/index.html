Artifice · Basic

Shell Converter

Item details

Cost500c
Burden1
Crafting points
30
Crafting discipline
Artificing

An artifice that easily converts fuel into shells. Less bulky to carry than an artifice kit, but cannot be used to repair artifices.

Treated as a kit for Burden purposes.

Rest Action: You can choose to create a small Artifice Shell, paying its cost in Magical Fuel to create.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Medium Capacity (20 points):
    You may also create Medium Shells (costs more Fuel still).

B mods

  • Increased Production (25 points):
    You may create two shells instead of 1 (costs more Fuel still).
  • Lightweight Design (80 points):
    This artifice is no longer counted as a kit and can thus be stored easily in non-combat inventory.
