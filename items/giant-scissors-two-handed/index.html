Equipment · Artisan Weapon

Giant Scissors (Two-Handed)

Item details

Cost1250
Burden2
Range5ft
Crafting points
30
Crafting discipline
Blacksmithing

Weapon effect

Shearing

You deal +1 damage against unarmored targets. 

Giant Scissors are used by Foragers in their activities.  With proficiency, they count as a 2h weapon that can be used as a foraging tool.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Elegant Executioner (35 points):
    Reduces Vivisect AP cost by 1.
  • Diamond Tip (35 points):
    Shearing now always deals +1 damage regardless of armor.

B mods

  • Executioner's Blade (50 points):
    Heavy basic attacks strike an adjacent target in range with a light attack. Once per round.
  • Separation (80 points):
    You can separate the Giant Scissors into two pieces for 0 AP (Quick), allowing you to dual wield them. You can use Separation again to put them back together. The passive changes from Shearing to Shearing Flurry which instead grants +1 accuracy to unarmored targets.
