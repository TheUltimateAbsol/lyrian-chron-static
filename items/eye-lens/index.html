Eye Lens

Item details

Cost500 Clim
Burden1
Crafting points
30
Crafting discipline
Artificing

A lens that boosts the power of mystic eyes when crafted with good materials. Has little effect normally, except for looking stylish.

If crafted with Tamahagane, Mithril, Orihalcum or Escudo gives a bonus to Mystic Eye attacks as if it was a weapon. 

This item is crafted similarly to an artifice weapon and gives the same sort of Material bonus, but cannot take alloys, universal mods or burden reducing effects.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Zoom In (20 points):
    Can function like binoculars. 

B mods

  • Focused Lens(25 points):
    When you deal damage with a mystic eye ability, you make immediately make a Light attack against one of the targets you damaged with it. This counts as your dual wield attack.
  • Silver Lens (80 points):
    Mystic Eye attacks made with this lens count as silver.
