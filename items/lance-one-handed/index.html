Lance (One-Handed)

Item details

30
Crafting discipline

Weapon effect

Charger Weapon

Can only be used while mounted. Counts as a polearm weapon and has all the same properties of one. Your first attack made with this weapon type after moving at least 20ft deal maximum damage instead of rolling.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Crown Tip (10 points):
    The first basic attack per round made immediately after moving 50ft or more, dismounts mounted targets on damage.
  • Demi-Lance (20 points):
    Can be used while dismounted as a normal spear, but loses its movement max damage bonus unless mounted.

B mods

  • Justice Lance (50 points):
    Your movement speed is capped at 25ft while this is in your burden. This weapon has an additional 5ft range and a 15ft threaten range.
  • Shield Lance (80 points):
    When spending at least 3 AP to move in one turn, your next basic defense before the start of your next turn costs 1 less RP.
