Equipment · Artisan Weapon

Sickle

Item details

Cost500 Clim
Burden1
Range5ft
Crafting points
30
Crafting discipline
Blacksmithing

Sickle's are used by Farmers in their activities. With proficiency, they are treated as a One-Handed axe that can be used as a farmer's tool.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Reinforced Langet (10 points):
    This weapon cannot be disarmed.
  • Bearded Extension (20 points):
    Wild Swing now ignores guard, but the damage is reduced to 2 (light attack) or 4 (heavy attack).
  • Rotational Stability (35 points):
    If a target moves away from you when you attack with this weapon and your next attack this turn is a throwing attack using this weapon against that target, that attack gains lock on.

B mods

  • Boomerang Wing (25 points):
    After using a throwing attack with this weapon, you can return the weapon to your hand for 0 RP.
  • Lancebreaker (80 points):
    You gain a +2 Lancebreaker Power bonus against enemies wielding polearms or lances.
