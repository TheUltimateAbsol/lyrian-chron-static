Alchemy · Elixir

Souleater Elixir

Item details

Cost600 Clim
Burden1
Activation
1 AP
Crafting points
51
Crafting discipline
Alchemy

An elixir that heightens spirit circuits in the eyes, making the user able to view invisible creatures. When drank you take 11 true damage.  You gain the ability to see invisible (not hidden) creatures and gain a +10 bonus to Perception checks to detect creatures until the end of the encounter.

Crafting requirements

  • Materials: 1100 Alchemy units
  • 1x Rare Fire
  • 1x Common Water

Static edition of Lyrian Chronicles 0.13.1. Content and artwork sourced from Angel’s Sword Studios.
