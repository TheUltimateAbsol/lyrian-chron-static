A small handheld device that display the astra level of where you're currently standing.

Customization

Mods

A mods

  • Bag Clip (10 points):
    The device can be used by attaching it to a belt or bag and no longer requires to be held in your hands.

B mods

  • Item Checker (25 points):
    You can use the device to check objects to determine if they are corrupted with astra or safe to touch.
  • Increased Capacity (50 points):
    When activated, the device now remains active for a week on the same shell.
  • Radar (80 points)
    While active, points to the highest source of non-ambient astra within a 1 mile range.
