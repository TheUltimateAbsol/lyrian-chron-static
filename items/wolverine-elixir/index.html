Wolverine Elixir

Item details

Cost1250 Clim
Burden1
Activation
1 AP
Crafting points
58
Crafting discipline
Alchemy

An elixir that prevents non-lethal injuries.  When drunk, you take 11 true damage. Until the end of the encounter, you do not suffer any injuries, except for a Mortal Wound (you can still lose HP and still become downed when reaching 0 HP). 

Crafting requirements

  • Materials: 2050 Alchemy units
  • 1x Mystic Earth
  • 1x Rare Fire

Static edition of Lyrian Chronicles 0.13.1. Content and artwork sourced from Angel’s Sword Studios.
