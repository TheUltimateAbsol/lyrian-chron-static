Identity & lore

Lineage overview

Salamanders are a proud, fiercely independent race, valuing personal strength and mastery over their innate fire magic. They are artisans at heart, channeling their elemental gifts into crafts that demand intense heat and fine control, such as metalwork, glassblowing, and the forging of enchanted items. In Sorthen, where these arts are highly respected, Salamanders have carved out a niche for themselves as master crafters, known for producing items of rare beauty and resilience.

While they have a somewhat insular culture, valuing Salamander kinship and traditions, they are not unwelcoming to outsiders. In Glassteps, their primary settlement, Salamanders coexist harmoniously with Demons and other races.

Salamander society places a strong emphasis on self-expression through craft. Each Salamander seeks to leave a mark on the world through their creations, which often hold personal or spiritual significance. Their apprenticeships are long and arduous, as young Salamanders must learn to temper their fiery nature and master their powers before they are considered full artisans. This mastery is symbolized by the ritual creation of a “Soulforge,” a unique piece of art that captures the essence of the Salamander’s inner fire and serves as a testament to their skill and control.
