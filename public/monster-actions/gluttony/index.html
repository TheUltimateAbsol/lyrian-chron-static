Monster active action

Gluttony

2 AP

Range: 10ft

Keywords: Rapid

You attempt to consume a target, making a Heavy Attack against the target.  On hit, the target is affected by Gluttony and becomes engulfed in slime.  An engulfed target becomes Weakened.  An ally can attempt to pull a consumed target from the slime with a DC20 Power Check, removing the effects of Gluttony.  

Used by monsters

MonsterGiant Prismatic Slime
