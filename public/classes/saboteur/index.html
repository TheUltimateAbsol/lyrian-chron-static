Identity & lore

Class overview

Rogues that create opportunities for their teammates, breaking through and creating their own entrances while hindering the enemy.

Tactical guidance

Playing Saboteur

Saboteurs combine scouting tools with some gadgets and explosives they can use in combat.

Presence Concealment protects you from certain magical tracking tools. Scouting Ahead is useful when sneakily trying to move through a dungeon or other dangerous place, allowing your group to move stealthily even when not everyone in the group has put points into stealth.

Chemical Explosive is a bomb that can be used to blow up many different things. Blowing a hole through a wall, or just blowing up a house straight up are both possible. You can only use it once per arc, so you do need to put in a bit of thought before committing to it. It can deal a lot of damage if it hits a person, but the detonation time is too long for it to see use in combat.

Flash Bomb is effectively a free flashbang flask that allows a save. Daze is pretty powerful when you can inflict it on multiple people, tossing a flash bomb every turn can be worth it, even against just a single enemy.

Wire Sweep is another potentially powerful CC tool. Because it is a save instead of an attack, enemies with low toughness may find it hard to defend against.

Ability

Chemical Explosive

4 AP
Range
5ft
Keywords
Sure Hit

You place a Chemical Explosive package that detonates after one minute. The explosion has a 15 ft radius and deals Double Heavy damage. The package makes an obvious noise after it has been planted that can be heard within the radius. The explosive charge deals 5x damage against inanimate objects. You can create a Chemical Explosive during an interlude phase for free, but can only carry one at a time. The check to disable a Chemical Explosive is equal to 20 + 2 x Power of the creator.
