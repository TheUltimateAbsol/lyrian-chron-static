Tactical guidance

Playing Wildheart

Wildheart is a continuation of the animal summoner that gets more powerful animals and more control over them. Expanded Menagerie lets you choose which animal you want to summon in exchange for 1 mana, which is well worth for not having to rely on RNG. 

You gain two buffing tools for your pet as Wildheart. The first being X-Attack, which gives a +1 focus and power buff for a moderate AP cost. With this buff, pets become a more viable dpser and target for Bestial Command. Secret Art: Bestial Wrath pushes this even further (but doesn't stack), giving a +2/+2 and letting pets compete offensively with dps characters. It also makes them not die once, letting them potentially soak another hit if focused.

Quick Revive is a situational ability that's there for if you mess up or your pet gets hit by an instakill attack. With it, you don't get locked out of your pet abilities for the entire fight if something like that happens.

Leashing is a cheap teleport for your pet that you can use to pull it back to safety, or to help it advance without spendin its own AP.

Secret Art

Your summon gains Bestial Wrath and AP equal to its maximum AP they may immediately use. Bestial Wrath gives +2 Focus and +2 Power and prevents the summon from dropping below 1 HP. This effect lasts for 3 rounds or until the summon is reduced to 1 HP.
