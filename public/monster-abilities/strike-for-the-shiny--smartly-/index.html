Monster ability

Strike for the Shiny, Smartly!

You are predisposed to attack targets who are displaying the most valuable items, Crafter numerical bonuses (Focus, Power, Crafting skill) are not perceptible but things like layering for armor or alloying weapons are, You prioritize value on weapons and artifacts, but if none of that is visible then you will default to attacking the one with the shiniest armor. You also consider current health, attempting to down (not finish off, just down) low health >25% HP targets as top priority, it’s easier to loot people who can’t fight back after all!
