Ability

Blinding Dust

1 RP2 MP

Range: 5ft

Keywords: Sidestep,Blinded,Circuit

You flap your wings and blow dust in the eyes of the attacker, making them Blinded 6 until the end of their attack. If their attack against you misses, you may Sidestep 5ft.

Requirements

You are the target of an attack.

Unlocked by classes & races

Sub-raceMothfolk
