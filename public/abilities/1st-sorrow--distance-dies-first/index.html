Ability

1st Sorrow: Distance Dies First

2 AP2 MP

Range: 60ft

Keywords: Teleport,Trick Attack,Isolated

You teleport behind the target and make a Heavy attack against them.

If you would be isolated after the teleport, the range increased to 120ft.

Requirements

Must be in Lone Wolf Stance. Must be made with a Katana, Heavy Blade, Scythe or Small Weapon.
