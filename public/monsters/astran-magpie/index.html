Strategy

The astran magpie follows the targeting order described in its Strike of the Shiny ability. It will use Swooping Strike exclusively.

Running this monster

A large group of astran magpies + 1 or 2 greater astran magpie's make a decent challenge for a group of adventurers. Due to the tendency to go for shiny armored targets, they can be a lot less scary if the group has a heavy tank and no person wearing valuables.

Gather Parts: 50u of feathersteel from their talons.
