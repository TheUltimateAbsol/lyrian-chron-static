Monster · Grunt Undead

Skeleton

Skeletons are the bones of people long dead that have been reanimated, usually through necromancy but rarely through astra corruption. They are mindless undead, swinging a weapon - often times a bone of themselves - at the closest living creature they see.

Running this monster

The skeleton is a very weak monster, especially against groups that possess bludgeoning attacks. A large group of skeletons is needed to provide a good challenge for a group of adventurers.
