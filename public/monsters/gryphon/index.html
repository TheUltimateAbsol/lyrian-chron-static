Gryphon

The gryphon despite its appearance is an animal that naturally appears in the wild. They are territorial, but not hostile to people that stay far enough away from them.

Appearance
The gryphon is a large creature has the body of a lion with the wings and head of an eagle.
Habitat
Found in the mountains or other places with high elevation.

Strategy

The gryphon will stay in the sky and harrass with Wind Blast until its mana runs out. If it lands a hit and knocks a target prone, it will follow up with Dive Attack on the target.

If the enemy has a lot of ranged attackers or if they are too spread out for a Wind Blast, the gryphon might go in earlier with a Dive Attack and use regular attacks instead.

Running this monster

A group of gryphons can challenge a group of seasoned adventurers.

Gather Parts: 500 units of Direhide.
