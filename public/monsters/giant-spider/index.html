Habitat
The giant spider is most commonly found underground or in caves where a broodmother has set up her lair.

Strategy

The Giant Spider will attempt to Web Spray the closest target if it is not in melee range. Afterwards, it will move up and attack using poison bite whenever it can.

Running this monster

A large group of giant spiders can provide a decent challenge to a group of adventurers. Should be combined with other grunt spiders to create a more varied encounter.
