Astral Tiger

The astral tiger is a tiger that has been corrupted by Astra. It phases in and out of reality at seeming random times, though the beast itself always seem perfectly aware of how it will move.

Strategy

An astral tiger’s main strategy is to use Desync to reposition out of range of their melee attackers or to approach ranged attackers. They will prioritize jumping on people with low HP or RP to hit them with their Resync Bite.

Running this monster

Three to four astral tigers is decent challenge for a group of adventurers.
