Strategy

The wolves will try to swarm a target, though they won't take attacks of opportunity to do so. They will keep using topple prey until the target is prone, then the other wolves will use go for the throat.

Running this monster

A large group of wolves will make a good challenge for a group of adventurers, ideally led by an Alpha Wolf. All wolves move at the same time to let them gain a huge bonus to their hit rolls before attacking.
