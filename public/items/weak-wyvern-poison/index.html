Alchemy · Poison

Weak Wyvern Poison

Contact Poison. Can be applied to a weapon. Lasts for 1 hits or 1 minute (6 rounds).

When an attack with this poison damages an enemy, they become poisoned. The next time they move the full distance they can in an action they take, they take 3d6 full pierce Poison damage.
