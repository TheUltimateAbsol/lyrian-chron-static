Equipment Patching Kit

Contains all the necessary materials to jury rig a fix on damaged weapons.

Rest Action: You can patch a broken piece of equipment or adventuring essential item, allowing it to be usable until the end of the scenario if it is repairable. The equipment still counts as broken at the end of the scenario and cannot be patched again until fully repaired.
