Version 0.13.3

Latest update

Rulebook

  • Skybound Trial added to the rules
    • Skybound tier is now 10k+ (skill point increase at 5k unaffected)
    • To go past 10k, a challenge must now be undertaken.
  • Added some kit rules for storing picked up kits.
  • Fall damage prone wording slightly changed to be based on fall dmg taken rather than pure distance.
  • Escaping the map rules updated slightly to not disallow defensive actions before escape.

Races

  • Nio are now immune to Petrification.
  • Pigfolk Break Piggy Bank now costs 0 AP instead of 0 RP.

Classes

  • NEW:
    • Aetherologist, a tier 3 caster class.
    • Enforcer, a tier 2 shotgun tank class
    • Fateweaver, a tier 2 healer class.
  • Aetherie
    • Spirit Circuit Crash now also disables upkeep that costs mana.
  • Anti-Mage
    • Can now take agility as stat option.
  • Armiger
    • Full Dimensional Equip no longer swaps out your cloak unless your assigned set you're swapping to also has a cloak.
  • Battlemaster
    • Disarming Attack now is a Heavy attack.
  • Bodyguard
    • VIP protection attacks now gain Crushing against you. However, you may spend 1 mana to remove this effect for 1 round.
  • Deadeye
    • Can now be accessed through Fusilier.
  • Evil Eye
    • All AoE eye abilities now force you to focus on a target as the center of the cone (no more side-eyeing).
  • Executor
    • Silentium now has the Divine keyword.
  • Fencer
    • Parry now is a light attack parry against light attacks. Overcharge added to make it a Heavy attack again.
  • Fusilier
    • Wording Updates
  • Iai Style
    • Key ability wording fix.
  • Mage Knight
    • Clarified mounted casting.
  • Monk
    • Flowing Kick combo now also increases the mana cost by 1 (turning it into a 1 AP, 1 Mana heavy).
  • Mycomancer
    • Fixed wording on poison spore to make it compatible for larger creatures.
  • Mystic Eyes of Petrification
    • All abilities now correctly have the mystic eye keyword.

Items

  • NEW:
    • Breathing Mask
    • Current Fins
  • Added Sling A mod.
  • Fixed wording on grappling hook Emergency Reel mod.
  • Spiked Shield now also deals elemental damage if shield is alloyed with an element.
  • Bubble Flow Armor mod cost increased, now lets you hold your breath indefinitely.

Breakthroughs

  • NEW:
    • Navy Seal breakthrough for Selkie.
  • Large Specimen can now be taken by male oni.
  • Divine Luck II wording updated.
  • Organized Inventory III can now be taken multiple times.
