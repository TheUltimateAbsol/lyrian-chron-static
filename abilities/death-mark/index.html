Ability

Death Mark

No listed cost

Range: Sight

Keywords: Encounter Start,Killing Blow,Sidestep

You mentally mark a target for death by applying Death Mark, opening them up to your Assassin abilities. 

On Killing Blow against a target marked by Death Mark, you may Sidestep up to your Speed and take the Hide action for 0 RP.

Requirements

Enemy is surprised.
