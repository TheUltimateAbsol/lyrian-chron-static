Ability

Faerie Light Eyes: Hold Person

1 RP1 MP

Range: 60ft

Keywords: Illusion,Mind Affecting,Stun,Mystic Eye

You gaze into the target’s eyes and stun them for 1 turn, reducing their AP and RP to 0. While stunned, the target can also not take any actions.

Requirements

Target must be a grunt and have eyes.
