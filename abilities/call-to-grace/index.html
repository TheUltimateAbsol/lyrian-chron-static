Ability

Call to Grace

0 RP

Range: 60ft

Keywords: Sanctify

You trigger the effect of Sanctify, healing the target of the attack by your sanctify amount (and reducing the sanctify stacks). If the attack targets multiple creatures, you only heal one.

Requirements

An enemy within range that inflicted with Sanctify is attacking an ally.
