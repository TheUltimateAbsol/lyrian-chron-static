Ability

Faerie Flash II

1 AP1 MP

Range: -

Keywords: Teleport

You Teleport up to twice your Speed to an unoccupied space that you can see.

If you gain an effect that would let you regain the use of Faerie Flash and you use Faerie Flash II that turn, instead reduce the mana cost of this ability by 1 (to a minimum of 0).
