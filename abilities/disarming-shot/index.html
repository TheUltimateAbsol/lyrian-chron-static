Ability

Disarming Shot

2 AP2 MP

Range: Ranged Weapon Range

Keywords: Disarm

You may make a Heavy Attack against the target. If the attack beat the passive evasion of the target (before Dodge bonuses), you Disarm the target until the start of your next turn. The target may spend 1 RP to avoid the disarm effect. This is in addition to any RP the target may spend to avoid the attack itself.
