Ability

Fireball Barrage

3 AP

Range: 60ft

Keywords: Fire,Spell,Scatter

You hurl three Fireballs at three separate 15x15ft areas, making a Light Attack that deals Fire Damage against all units in each area. Each unit can only be attacked once per use of this ability, even if hit by multiple fireballs.
