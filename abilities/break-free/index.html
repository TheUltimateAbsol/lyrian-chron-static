Ability

Break Free

0 RP

Range: Self

Keywords: Rage

You may use this ability when you would be affected by a Stun, Root, Fear or a similar effect that would prevent you from acting that's inflicted by an enemy. You may immediately enter Rage Stance without spending any AP or RP. You gain immunity to this effect for the duration of the Rage.

Requirements
