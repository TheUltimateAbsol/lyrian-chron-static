Key ability

Fungal Metabolite

Benefit 1

Due to symbiotic fungi within your body, you become resistant to all commonly known (book) poisons and may use Resist Poison.

Benefit 2

After being infected by an exotic poison or disease and surviving, that poison/disease is added to your list of resistances. Any disease or poison that you are resistant to can be used with Amoxicillin.

Benefit 3

Against a poison you are resistant to, you may use Resist Poison for 0 RP, 1 Mana instead of 1 RP.
