Aerial Mage

Benefit 1

You may use Aerial Mode Switch.

Benefit 2

While grounded and riding an Aerial Stave, you may reload 2 shells instead of 1 when taking the Reload Artifice basic action on your staff. You may do this even if your hands would normally be full and can draw both shells with 1 action.

Aerial Mode Switch

0 AP
Granted byAerial Mage
RangeSelf
Keywords
QuickRapidAerial Mage

You switch between hover and aerial mode.

Hover mode allows you to attack with normal attacks with one hand. Your airships movement speed is reduced to 10ft while in hover mode. 

While in Aerial Mode you cannot use any attacks or actions that are not airship weapons or have the Aerial Mage keyword.

Requirements

Riding Aerial Staff.
