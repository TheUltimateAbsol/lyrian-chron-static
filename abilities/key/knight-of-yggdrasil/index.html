Knight of Yggdrasil

Benefit 1

You may use Fluttering Blade.

Benefit 2

Once per round, if an enemy is holding a weapon affected by your Fluttering Blade, you may spend 1 RP to deal Heavy Full Pierce damage to it.

Benefit 3

When a Fluttering Blade you created is within 5ft of you, you may spend 0 AP to grab hold of it with a free hand.

Unlocked ability

Fluttering Blade

0 AP
Granted byKnight of Yggdrasil
Range120ft
Keywords
QuickRapidActive

You infuse a melee weapon within ability range that you are familiar with (belongs to you and was originally in your combat inventory at the start of the encounter, or has been in your dimensional storage for at least a day) with magic, causing it to float into the air that listens to your directions. You can have one Fluttering Blade at a time. 

When a Fluttering Blade weapon takes damage, it drops to the ground and becomes inert.

Fluttering Blade has a base evasion of 7+Your Agility.

You can direct your Fluttering Blade to move, it has a base movement speed of 30ft and does not provoke attacks of opportunity by moving. You can also direct your Fluttering Blade to attack within its attack range. It can make basic Heavy, Light and Precise attacks as well as special Fae Knight attacks. The blade is counted as its own entity for the purpose of attacking, though it uses your stats.

You need at least 1 hand free to direct a blade.
