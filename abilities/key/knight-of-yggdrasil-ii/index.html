Unlocked ability

Fae Knight Style: Willow Stance

0 AP
Granted byKnight of Yggdrasil II
RangeSelf
Keywords
QuickStance

You enter Fae Knight Style: Willow Stance. Increasing your Fluttering Blade Limit to 2. Shields can be used as Fluttering Blades while in Willow Stance. 

For 2 mana, all Fluttering Blades created in Willow Stance this turn can be enhanced with Heavy Spin, allowing them to be used for some Willow abilities but they no longer gain your agility as extra evasion.  Heavy Spin blades also deal 5d6 upgraded heavy attack damage, even if they were originally a one-handed weapon.

When directing your Heavy Spin blades to move, you may direct two blades for 1 AP instead of just 1 blade.

Unlocked by classes
