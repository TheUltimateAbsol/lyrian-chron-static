Key ability

Puppet Grandmaster

Benefit 1

You can summon puppets under your control using Summon Piece. While these puppets follow movement rules as described in the abilities and are always Medium size, they can appear as whatever you wish, decided when you take this class.

Benefit 2

As an EP action, you may change the appearance of your pieces.

Unlocked ability

Summon Piece

1 AP
Granted byPuppet Grandmaster
Range60ft

You summon a Pawn puppet under your control. It (and other puppets from this class) have HP equal to 20 + Focus, no evasion and no guard. These puppets are not living targets and are generally treated as structures rather than creatures.

They last until the end of the encounter.

Overcharge: You may spend extra mana to summon the following puppets instead:

1 Mana: Knight or Bishop

2 Mana: Rook

3 Mana: Queen
