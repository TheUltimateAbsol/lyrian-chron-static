Key ability

Acolyte's Journey

Grants+5 skill pointsReligion

Benefit 1

You gain proficiency in light armor, shields and a single common group of weapons. This group may include Channeling Weapons, even though they aren't consider common weapons. (But you only get 1 of the possible channeling weapons).

Benefit 2

You gain +5 skill points in the Religion skill. You can exchange any skill point for 2 expertise points, but must spend them in this skill.

Benefit 3

You are welcome at establishments for your religion and they are obligated to help you with lodging, advice, food and sometimes equipment.

Benefit 4

Upon Mastery of the class, you gain Elemental Mastery in your chosen Divine's element (or Holy if just human).

Unlocked by classes
